- **2026-11-09, footsteps from the feet, and by surface.** Brief (from the playtest list): steps out of step with the walk and run; each ground its own steps. **Before:** `Yours.cs` played a step every 0.75 m you moved (1.05 running, 0.55 sneaking), three sounds (soft, hard, boards); nobody else had steps. **Decided: footfall marks found in the clips at load, not key frames in the .glb and not foot heights read each frame.** `godot/World3D/Footfalls.cs` works out each stepping clip's ankle heights over 240 samples by forward kinematics through the clip's own bone tracks (rests where a bone has none), on the clips' skeleton; a foot is down within 10% of its lift (at least 1.5 cm) over its lowest, and in the air again only above 35% (hysteresis: the sprint's right foot shuffles in the band and gave three touch-downs without it). Each frame a model's playhead is compared with the last frame's (`Watch`, the short way round the loop): a touch-down mark crossed going forward is a step; going backward (backing off plays the clip reversed) the lift-off marks are the touch-downs. Why not the alternatives: key frames or a method track would need retarget.py and a re-export, and must be kept in step by hand when the clips change (these follow them); reading the drawn feet each frame sees the cross-fade blends and samples a run's one- or two-frame contact at whatever moment a frame falls (misses and doubles). The marks are exact, need no Blender, cost a dictionary lookup a model a frame, and don't care about the rate (clips are sped up, slowed down and reversed), the frame rate or the LOD. For the 0.25 s a clip change cross-fades no steps come (the drawn feet are still the old clip's; the new clip starts at its 0, often a mark: the self-test caught one 43 cm in the air). Marks found: walk (1.33 s) R 0.61 L 1.28; jog (0.93) L 0.00 R 0.47; sprint (0.67) R 0.33 L 0.64; crouch (2.0) R 0.96 L 1.95; against the curves, the walk's left ankle reaches its floor at 1.29-1.31 s, where the foot also stops moving forward, so the marks are within ~20 ms of the heel strike. **Who steps:** your own body (People.UpdateYou, through `Yours`: plain players, at the ear) and every model People draws (the nearest 40 of the living and the dead within 40 m; capsules and the dormant don't), through Sounds' 3D voices from the foot: priority 0 (anything else takes their voice), at most 5 at once, heard within 20 m, +6 dB over yours, muffled like the rest. Level and pitch by gait (crouch -22 dB, walk -11, jog -7, sprint -4). **Surfaces** (`godot/Audio/SynthSteps.cs`, replacing SynthWorld.Step): one kind a ground, `step_grass` ... `step_concrete`, and `step_water` (11 kinds x 4 variants; making all 79 sounds now ~580 ms on the worker, was ~330). Each is a heel and the ball of the foot 40-70 ms later exciting that ground: grass a soft thud and blades brushing; dirt a gritty crunch; field a low crunch and stalks; forest leaf litter (tiny clicks, every other variant a twig); road a heel click and grit; paving a click and a slab rocking; concrete a sharp click and a sandy scrape; tiles a bright click, a ring and the room giving it back; boards a hollow knock, every third a creak; bridge a deep hollow boom and a loose plank; water a splash, bubbles and a trickle. The ground is the tile under the foot (`Yours.Surface`): a bridge is a bridge; water if the tile is water or a water tile is within 0.3 m of the foot (the bank: water itself is impassable, so the shallows are the bank's edge). Band energies (Goertzel, 100 Hz-6.4 kHz) looked at: each its own shape. **Checked:** `./check.sh --all` green; the self-test's step checks now (62 checks in all): walking, running (steps quicker, jog or sprint) and backing off, every step on a foot that's down (the clip's ankle within 5 cm of its floor, the drawn one no higher than the other): walking 426 ms apart (the jog clip: walking speed is past the walk clip's 2.2 m/s), running 283 ms (sprint), backing off by the reversed clip; a zombie walking at you is heard stepping from its feet; each ground gives its own kind on a little grid (water, the bank, a step back from it, a bridge beside it) and every kind's sound differs. Traces: `STEP_TRACE=1` prints each clip's marks and each of your steps (clip, playhead, rate, the clip's and the drawn ankles' heights); `STEP_SHOT=prefix` saves a picture at your 4th-6th steps. With `--hold=w` and `--hold=shift,w` the stepping ankle is 8-15 cm up (the ankle is 9 cm over the sole) and the other 40-53 cm, each step within a frame of its mark; the pictures from outside (`--eye`) show the front foot planted and the back leg up. Previews: `user://sounds-preview/step_<ground>_<n>.wav` (the old step_soft/hard/wood ones deleted there). **Not checked:** by ear, any of it (levels, the dead's steps against the moans, whether a crowd's feet are too much or too little); walking speed sits just past the walk clip's range, so plain walking plays the jog clip (People's thresholds, unchanged); crawling (you prone, the dead crawling) is silent (the crawl clip isn't marked); steps lost in the 0.25 s of a clip change.
