- **2026-11-10, the people and the dead move properly.** The player asked to improve the human and zombie animations.
  Worst faults first: walking played the jog clip, every gait slid its feet, the dead all walked alike, crawlers were
  silent, the woken dead had no get-up, hits and bites were a punch clip, weapon actions halted at contact, the jump
  flung its arms, nothing knew a limb was gone. Six commits (870e87e, 1d5aa43, a0ea7b2, ebc26be, feee953, and this
  one's docs), each with a measurement before and after (experiments/animation/).
  - **Why / what was tried.** Measured first, on the world's own logic (new: godot/Characters/AnimTest.tscn, the pz
    model played as People.cs plays it, the drawn feet measured): the planted foot slid 37-46 cm a step at every
    speed. The library's clips are far faster than People.cs assumed (walk 1.1 m/s at rate 1 against an assumed
    1.4; the "jog" 6.1, the sprint 9.2 against 3.0 and 5.0), and the rates were clamped to 0.5-1.6. Rates alone
    can't fix it: the library's planted foot isn't steady (it slides back 0.3 m/s mid-stance and scuffs forward at
    toe-off). Also found: retargeted, every library foot points 31 deg toes-down, the ball ~5 cm under the ground.
    So the clips are derived in Blender (art/scripts/derive_clips.py): stride warping by leg IK for the speeds, then
    bake-time planting (each foot pinned while down, travelling at the clip's pace; heel to ball carried on; landing
    and lifting held straight across for 3.5 cm; a run pinned on the ball, the heel kept up). A first planting pass
    that extended the pin into the approach frames drifted 20 cm and dropped the hips 58 cm (the brisk walk knelt in
    the pictures, which the slide numbers alone didn't show): the pin now starts at the true touch-down and runs
    backwards over the approach, capped at 8 cm. Gaits.cs measures each clip's speed at load (the median of the
    planted point's speed, so a scuff doesn't count) and picks the clip by speed bands (overlapping 8%).
    FootIK gained a ground function (the sim's heights; the world has no collision), steps for a held foot when the
    body stands or turns (opt-in: `Steps`; on by default it lifted the lab's walk-in-place feet 8 cm), and a clamp
    against cross-fades sinking a foot more than 2 cm. The lab's tests match their committed runs after each commit
    (iktest, walktest lock-on, handtest, cartest, ragtest; walktest lock-off rows move 1-3 cm between runs anyway).
    Foot slide per step (cm), before -> after: 0.8 m/s 37.7 -> 4.7; 1.4 36.7 -> 4.9; 2.2 41.6 -> 2.6; 2.5 45.9 -> 3.1;
    3.5 41.8 -> 0.7; 4.4 43.6 -> 5.7; 5.0 46.0 -> 5.2; sneaking 102 -> 6.2; 8 deg down 41.2 -> 20.8 mid tier, 4.4 near
    (IK); the walker 31 -> 4.9-18 by gait (the drags count their scuff); the runner 49.7 -> 0.9; the crawl 87 -> 4.1
    (hands); a lost leg 48.7 -> 1.0 (hop). Stopping 39.7 -> 16.4 mid, 11.5 near; turning on the spot 47.6 -> 39.7 mid,
    12.1 near. Weapon actions (--actioncurve): the bat at contact 0.1 -> 7.4 m/s (2% -> 100% of its peak); the knife
    halted at full reach before, 67% of its peak where it meets a body now; hands on grips 0.0-0.1 cm. Jump: hands at
    most 1.41 -> 0.90 m apart, no longer over the head. A lost arm's stump strays 18 -> 1 deg from held while walking.
    One of the dead with its target 90 deg to its left: face 93 -> 27 deg off it.
