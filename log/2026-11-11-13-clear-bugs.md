- **2026-11-11, the clear bugs from the quality pass's list.** The pass (log 11) listed what it would not change because it changed behaviour; the player asked for the clear bugs among them fixed: its items 1, 3, 4 and 9.
  - **Fixed.**
    - **A command runs at its own world's day length** (1). `Commands.applyCommand` sets the clock's scale from the state first, as `Tick.tick` does (free when unchanged). A command before the first tick after a load, or at the start of a replay (which goes through `applyCommand`), no longer runs at the scale the last world left.
    - **A refused command changes nothing** (4). A refused `work` turned you and wrote the reason as your note; a refused `use` turned you. Neither is logged, so a replay had you facing another way. Now a refusal puts your facing back and writes no note. The note was how the player saw why R did nothing, so the game shows the reason itself (`GameSession.Command`: a short toast for a refused `work`).
    - **An unknown command is refused** (9). `Commands.execute` threw for a type it didn't know (and, before reaching that, for any tile command sent without a tile): both are a reject now, so an old log with a command since removed fails its replay with a reason, not a crash. `Talk.check` returns "not done" for a task kind it doesn't know.
    - **`talk` with nobody named picks the nearest by the distance its range is in** (9): tiles with a diagonal counting one (`Talk.apart`; steps break ties). Someone two away on the diagonal no longer loses to someone three away straight ahead and out of range.
    - **Someone lost on an expedition or at an outpost takes all they carried** (3): `Expeditions.lose` (`Inventory.loseAll`: the worn slots, the bag, the pockets of either; and their bed is freed, which only the outpost did). The bag used to stay in the item list carried by nobody.
    - **Items given, traded or burnt as fuel leave through `Items.removeItem`** (3): Outposts.cs (the truck's fuel), Settlements.cs (a gift), Visitors.cs (a trade). A bag traded away leaves its contents behind.
    - **`Outposts.updateOutposts` walks a copy of the list** (3): an outpost whose last member died was taken off the list being walked, and the next outpost missed that dawn.
    - **A friendly settlement finds the spot before it sends the person** (3): with nowhere for a newcomer to turn up it keeps them, and the settlements after it still have their turn.
  - **Not touched** (on the same list, but choices): what you carry into a new game+ (`Escape.carried` leaves the bag out); the removed-command names `spawnVehicle` and `toggleDoor`.
  - **Checked.** `./check.sh --all` green (949 tests, self-test 113). `RefusalsAndLossesTest` (7: one per fix; the outpost test plays the same dawn with the first outpost's member hurt and killed and compares what the second sends home). Solo balance, seeds 1 to 6: byte for byte the lines from before. **Not run:** world balance (the colony player sends expeditions and deals with settlements, so its lines may move); nobody has played it.
