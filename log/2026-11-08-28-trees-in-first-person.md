- **2026-11-08, trees in first person.** Asked for: weigh the approaches, pick one, build it (docs/trees.md has the comparison). **Picked:** modelled low-poly trees from a headless Blender script with alpha-cut leaf cards on the near LOD only; no impostors (fog ends the view at ~50 m, where 50-triangle meshes cost nothing), no CC0 pack (no download needed), no runtime generation (the script is the art pipeline's way and gives a file to look at). **Art:** art/scripts/build_trees.py (~4 s): spruce, pine, broadleaf, birch and a dead snag, three LODs each (near ~1-1.35k triangles, mid 94-488, far 10-86), a bush and a fern; vertex colours with cheap occlusion; crown normals out of the crown (soft, mass-lit crowns); a 1024 leaf atlas (broadleaf, needles, fern, birch) painted by numpy. Out: godot/assets/trees/trees.glb (0.3 MB; import with LOD generation and tangents off) and leaves.png (0.2 MB; mipmaps, VRAM-compressed). **godot/World3D/Trees.cs:** one MultiMesh per chunk, species and LOD (visibility ranges to the chunk's centre: 0-24, 24-44, 44+ m), 4 chunks built a frame, rebuilt when a chunk's tree tiles or forest ground change; species by a hash of the tile in ragged 6x6 stands, size 0.7-1.2, turn, tint and sway phase by hash; the trunk within 12 cm of the tile's centre (the sim's tile is the collider); a shader: vertex colour times tint, the atlas with alpha scissor on cards, bark furrows from noise, sway (bend with height squared, ~14 cm at 10 m) and flutter. Undergrowth on forest ground without a tree: ferns and bushes, knee-high, drawn to 30 m, no shadows; no scattered small trees (a sapling you walk through reads as a bug). godot/World3D/Terrain.cs: `Terrain.Height(x, z)`, flat, for the heightmap. WorldMesh.Thing: the tree boxes are gone (one line). FirstPerson: builds Trees; the debug panel's 3D line counts trees.
