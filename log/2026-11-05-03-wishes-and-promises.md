- **2026-11-05, wishes and promises (wishes.ts, schema 58):** one small want per survivor at a time, Sims 3 style. On the hour, someone without one gets one 20% of the time (a hash of who and when), picked from those that fit them now: a hot meal (there's a stove), a night in a real bed (slept on the floor, or comfort under 50), a coat (autumn, winter, a nuclear winter; none on), a weapon (guards without one), to see someone (whoever you control if they don't mind you, else the person they like best), to visit the graves (mourning, with a graveyard), a quiet day (after a death or an attack), to put one down (the hardened, or 3+ kills), company (social under 40), somewhere decent to sleep (comfort under 40). Each lasts a day, and is checked against what the sim already tracks, mostly thoughts gained since it began.
  Met: "got what they wanted" (+5 mood), +2 resolve. Promised (the Promise button in the survivor's Story panel) and met: "a promise kept" (+15 mood for 2 days), +8 resolve, +10 opinion of whoever you control. Promised and let run out: "a promise broken" (-10 for 2 days), -5 resolve, -10 opinion. Unpromised and run out: nothing. A hollow diamond over someone's head is a wish; a gold one, a promise. Nobody under your control has wishes.
