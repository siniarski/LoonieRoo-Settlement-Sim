- **2026-11-11, play fixes (UI): the night countdown, the map's stall, a death screen of its own.** Three things the player saw in a hand-played run.
  - **Night countdown.** Reproduced from the code: `Alerts.cs` printed `Night in {NIGHT_START - hour}h`, a raw double (`2.7833333333333314h`). Now `Alerts.untilText`: "2h 15m", "40m" under an hour (rounded up to the minute). The sweep of the HUD, cards, map and Colony tab for other doubles in strings found none unformatted: the other hours (`wish` hours, wanderers' `leaves in`, expedition `back in about Nh`, infection `Nh to cure`) go through `Math.Ceiling`/integer division first; `toFixed(1)` on food days. Test `alerts > the time to night is hours and minutes`. Picture: `experiments/play-fixes/ui/countdown.png` ("Night in 2h 14m").
  - **The stall.** A stopwatch in `Game._Process` (first-person update, HUD, map) with a probe that opened the map at frame 60 and closed it at 100, in a 1024 and a 2048 world. Closing the map costs only a catch-up frame (first-person update 21-28 ms, the chunks' build budget: it was not the stall). The stall is the map: `WorldMap.Rebuild` painted every tile with `SetPixel` and string tile lookups on the frame it opened and every 20 frames while open: 62-85 ms a frame at 1024 (5 ms at 256; about 4x that at 2048), so the screen hitched on the way into the map and on the way out of it each time it was re-opened. Now the picture is painted into a kept byte array 90,000 tiles a frame and uploaded once per pass (`WorldMap.Slice`); the first picture is made while you play, so the map opens on it, and later opens reuse the last one and refresh in slices. Frame at the open: 85 ms before, 16.7 ms after at 1024; flat 16.7 ms at 2048 (the map's own update 63 ms before, 7.7 ms worst slice after). Minimap and the 3D chunks left alone (not measured as a cost). Not verified: by hand in the player's own world.
  - **Death screen.** `Ui/DeathScreen.cs` replaces the HUD's succession panel: when `state.succession` is set (and no menu is over it) a full-screen panel says who died, of what, the day and time and where; with heirs it lists them (name, "was a priest in Brackley", resolve, where they are and how far from the body) with "Start anew" and "Just watch the colony"; with nobody left (`survivors.Count == 0`) it is the end ("The run came to N days and K of the dead put down", "Begin again, far from here", "Back to the menu", with nothing to resume). Keys 1-9 and Enter choose in the order shown, Esc the menu; every button works by click. The world is held for any succession (`AwaitingHeir` is now `succession != null`, so a death with no heir holds too: before, it played on under the panel), first-person input, mouse capture and the Colony tab's keys and mouse are off while it is up (`Game.DeathUp`). `Succession` gained `tick` and `kills` (the day on the screen, what they had killed). Self-test `SelfTestDeath.cs` (real input: the world held with W, a click and F ignored; key 1 chooses, a click chooses, the end form, the way to the menu, begin again) and `--deathshot=x.png`. Pictures: `death-screen.png`, `death-screen-end.png`.
