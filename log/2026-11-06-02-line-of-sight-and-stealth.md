- **2026-11-06, line of sight and stealth (branch sight-stealth).** System 5 of the six. New sight.ts.
  - Sight lines run tile to tile (Bresenham). Wall tiles and shut door tiles in between block them, and so does any border sight can't cross (seesThrough: edge walls, shut edge doors, boards; glass lets it through). A diagonal step needs a clear way round either corner. You see the far end even when it's a wall. The field of view is every tile within the radius with a clear line to it. The radius is SIGHT_RADIUS 8 by day and NIGHT_SIGHT 5 at night, plus the vigilant trait. From a tower you see over walls and roofs, out to TOWER_SIGHT.
  - Fog (fog.ts) now explores only what is in view, not a circle, so you don't learn what's inside a building by walking past it. A survivor's look is skipped when their tile, their sight radius, the walls (walkabilityVersion) and the open doors are all unchanged since last time. Then the field is the same and already explored, so the cache can't change any outcome, after a save and load too. The look also only traces lines to tiles not yet explored. That keeps it cheap: the busy bench is 0.72 ms against 0.86 ms before.
  - The dead notice you (zombies.ts plan, notices) if they see you or hear you, or if you're the one they were already after, never beyond AGGRO_RADIUS 20.
    - Sight: ZOMBIE_SIGHT 12 by day, 6 at night, halved if you're sneaking.
    - Hearing: your movement carries RUN_HEARD 8 tiles running, WALK_HEARD 4 walking, SNEAK_HEARD 1 sneaking, and nothing standing still. Colonists on the move are heard as walking.
    - Exceptions: night waves still know where everyone is (colony balance), and raiders still notice anyone within AGGRO_RADIUS.
    - So you can keep still behind a wall while one shuffles past, and one that never noticed you won't batter its way in. Loud things (noise.ts) still carry much further and draw them over.
  - Sneaking: C toggles it (the sneak command; control.sneaking). Half pace, no running, quiet, and half as visible. A blow to one of the dead that isn't after you (you snuck up, or it's busy with someone else) does SNEAK_ATTACK_PCT 300% damage ("hit it from behind").
  - Render: explored tiles out of view now are dimmed (palette unseen), and the people, the dead and animals in them aren't drawn. What's in view is visibleNow (sight.ts): your survivor's view when you control one, everyone's otherwise; it's derived and cached per tick, never saved. The debug overlay shows everything.
  - Tests changed to match: a static zombie has to see you, so the screamer test stands it closer, the herds test checks the zombie ignores you out of sight first, and the edge-door test has the zombie already after the one inside. tests/sight.test.ts (8).
