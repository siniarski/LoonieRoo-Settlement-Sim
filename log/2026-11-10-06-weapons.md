- **2026-11-10, weapons and firearms** (`cs/Sim/Systems/WeaponTable.cs`, `Shooting.cs`, `Weapons.cs`; `godot/World3D/Held.cs`). The player asked for a real set of weapons and firearms, sim first. A weapon is now an item kind of its own with its own numbers; shots are lines through the grid; there are commands to fire, reload and switch; the game draws each weapon, a muzzle flash and the rounds.

  - **Items.** New kinds: knife, hatchet, machete, crowbar, bat, pipe, sledge, spear, plank, pistol, revolver, shotgun, rifle, crossbow, and the boxes ammo (9mm, the old "box of rounds"), ammo38, shells, ammo308, bolts. `weapon` and `gun` stay valid for old saves, scenarios and the start (Worldgen's weapon): a `weapon` reads as a crowbar, a `gun` as a pistol (`Weapons.specOf(item)`); no schema bump. `wear`: a melee weapon's kills (breaks at its durability), a gun's rounds, a box's rounds. Boxes of a kind stack in the bag (`Inventory.stow` merges the rounds into one box).
  - **Shots** (`Shooting.cs`). A line from the shooter's centre along an aim, sideways error from a stateless hash of shooter, tick and pellet (no RNG draw: replays land the same). It runs tile by tile and stops at a wall, a shut door or a barricade on a tile, and an edge wall, a shut edge door or boarded window (`Pathfinding.seesThrough`: an unboarded window, whole or broken, lets it through). The first of the dead, the animals or residents whose body (96 SUB) it passes takes the hit, a miss carries on to what is behind; damage falls linearly from `effective` to `falloff` percent at range, nothing past it. A survivor of yours in the line before whatever you aim at holds your fire (the stray round never reaches them). Fixed: a kill by a stray round finishes any guard's job to hunt that zombie (`forgetZombie`).
  - **Commands** (all logged, replayed): `fire` (x, y: a direction, whole numbers up to 4096; first person sends its look), `reload` (a busy action counted in ticks: a magazine at once, or a round at a time while there is room and ammunition; shooting or walking off stops it), `switchWeapon` (the next weapon in the bag, or `itemId`; the old one goes into the bag). `swing` at a tile still works: a loaded gun fires along the line to the tile, anything else is a blow by the weapon's own reach, arc, time, endurance and noise. Keys: click fires with a loaded gun, **X** reload, **Q** next weapon.
  - **Guards.** They pick a gun only if ammunition for it is lying about (a spent gun is swapped for a club if there is none), else the best melee weapon (`GUN_PREFERENCE`, `MELEE_PREFERENCE`); they shoot with the same rays (`Weapons.guardShoot`: the gun's guard range, a clear line, none of ours in it, its fire rate; reloading from a box takes the gun's reload time: `Survivor.readyTick`, the one saved field added, 0 in an old save), swing by the weapon's own time and noise, hit with an axe or a hammer in the tool slot when they have no weapon.
  - **Noise.** A weapon has its own radius (`Noise.makeNoise(..., radius)`): crossbow 4, a blow 3-12, pistol 22, revolver 26, shotgun 32, rifle 36 (a turret's and the old gunfire stay 30). In a fresh 128-tile world one report wakes about 2.4 sleepers (pistol), 3.0 (shotgun), 3.2 (rifle) on average, at most 8 (`census noise`, cs/Harness/out/census-noise.txt); the awake static and herd zombies within the radius turn to look and herds within twice it turn towards it, so the cost of a shot is what is awake round you. A crossbow is quieter than a blow.
  - **Where they come from.** Loot tables changed (shares kept where they were; the old weapon, gun and ammo shares were split): kitchen (knife .08), bedroom (revolver .01, pistol .01, .38 .02, 9mm .04, knife .01; was gun .03, ammo .06), living (bat .03, crowbar .01), garage (crowbar .04, pipe .03, hatchet .01), bar (bat .05, knife .03), station (9mm .03, crowbar .04, pipe .03), police (pistol .10, revolver .02, shotgun .02, 9mm .18, .38 .04, shells .08, armour .14, crowbar .07, bat .05, knife .06, boots .10, bandage .14), hardware (hatchet .03, crowbar .01, taking from wood .25 -> .21), barn (shotgun .01, rifle .005, shells .015, .308 .01, taking from axe, hammer, scrap and cloth), shed (hatchet .02, from cloth); the scavenge table (crowbar .015, bat .01, knife .015, plank .01, 9mm .03, pistol .005, .38 .003, shells .002). Crafting: `weapon` and `gun` are gone as keys; bat (wood 2), plank (wood 2), spear (wood 2), bolts (wood 1) and crossbow (wood 3) need no knowledge, 9mm ammo, shotgun shells and pistols still need gunsmithing and a workshop (an old save's targets for `weapon` and `gun` are dropped).
  - **Balance** (150 seeds each way, 25 days, `docs/harness.md`; the scripted players now take the best weapon lying about, a gun only with its ammunition, reload, fetch ammunition and shoot at range, and the colony crafts 2 bats where it crafted 2 weapons).
    - Solo (150 seeds, apocalypse): mean day of death 3.67 -> 3.79 (paired +0.13 +- 0.10), median 4 both, none alive at 25. Deaths by zombies 36 -> 18, by starvation 99 -> 118 (they live to starve: the first days are still the food problem).
    - Colony (150 seeds): mean day the colony was wiped out or the last day 21.47 -> 21.51 (paired +0.04 +- 0.27), still alive at day 25: 12 -> 20 of 150.
    - Guns were in play: 96 of 100 colony runs had at least one gun lying about at some dawn (2.4 on average at the most), but rounds were few (29 at most on average): the first runs with guns removed from the loot were within 0.1 of those with them. So a gun is rare per find and ammunition is what is scarce.
