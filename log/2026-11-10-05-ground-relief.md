- **2026-11-10, the 3D ground has relief** (open-work "The 3D ground"). `Heights.relief()` returned 0: the ground was flat apart from rolling noise, although Terrain (organic world generation, log 2026-11-10-02) has an elevation and drainage. Heights now takes its large-scale shape from it: rivers run in valleys, a lake stands level in its hollow, the start is on its rise. Render only: no sim change, no schema change.
  - **Heights is render-only (checked).** Readers of `Heights` in `cs/Sim`: none (`TownIndex.cs` names `Heights.townness` in a comment); `cs/Harness/GenProfile.cs` hashes corners (a report); `godot/World3D/Terrain.cs` is the one hook the renderer reads it through (WorldMesh, FarField, Trees, Furniture, Items, Bodies, People, Animals, Roofs, FirstPerson, Sounds read `Terrain.*`). `Leaps.cs` says "the sim has no heights that block". Proof: the same world ticked 600 ticks (seeds 3 and 11, 192 tiles) on `main` and on this branch gives the same save file (sha256 prefix `5A6CC5563A5E42FC25EABA77` and `5C73D20960FA99D408D5FF2C`); a test (`relief > the sim's state does not depend on the ground`) ticks with and without reading heights and compares saves.
  - **What it does.** `relief(x, y)` = `RELIEF` (7 m) x Terrain's elevation (0..1, lattice of 16 tiles) eased between lattice points with smoothstep weights (no creases on lattice lines), from the start's level (the start is 0 on its rise). Terrain got two read-only accessors, `pointElevation`/`pointDepth` (lattice values). The water level (`waterAt`) is now the relief, plus over a hollow Terrain's fill level less the lake threshold (a lake's shore is where its ground meets it), less `WATER_DROP` (1.0 m): a function of the lattice only, so a lake is level (seed 6: 3.00 to 3.13 under zero across 28 tiles) and a river tilts with its valley (it ran on the rolling noise before). The wave (rolling noise: big octave 2.4 -> 1.6 m) fades toward water over 14 tiles (`WAVE_FADE`), land is lifted to the water's lip (surface + 0.15) if it would dip under it, and rises from the water no faster than 10% (`BANK_SLOPE`: a smoothed cone, so a bank is a beach not a wall). Chunk margin 10 -> 18 tiles so chunks agree; a chunk costs ~4.8 ms (was ~3 ms).
  - **Lots and streets.** Unchanged: a building sits on one level (mean of its footprint's corners and centre), flat over its footprint, the ground eases to it over 4 tiles. New: the ground one tile round a footprint (`APRON`) is exactly the level, so a door never opens on a step (test). Streets are not levelled on their own: they follow the relief and the lots beside them.
  - **Numbers** (4 worlds: seeds 1 and 42 at 512, 7 and 13 at 1024). Relief alone: 7 to 10 m from valley floor to the highest land, steepest 5 to 8% a tile, median ~1%. Whole ground corner to corner (not within 2 tiles of water): median 2.2%, p95 9%, p99 12%, max 0.5 (pads and banks). Open country (no town within 100, no water within 16): p95 5%, p99 7-8%, max 0.08-0.24. Along roads (centre to centre of consecutive road tiles): median 1.1%, p95 7.5%, p99 12-16%, p99.9 20-26%, max 0.34-0.39. Heights on seed 7 (512): start 0.04 m; the ground falls to -3.6 m at the river 80 tiles south; river water -3.9 to -4.15; seed 6 (256): lake at -3.0..-3.13, its bank from -2.1 (24 tiles out) to -2.75 (2 tiles out). Largest height difference in a 512 world, valley floor to the top, ~6.5-9 m on land corners (beds go 2-3 m under the water).
  - **Tried.** `WATER_DROP` .5 and .9 (water over the dry corner 8 tiles from a river up to 0.6 m; 1.0 with the smaller big octave: 0.34 at worst); a cone on lot levels (a pad beside water kept under the bank): worse roads, removed; `BUILDING_EASE` 8: worse roads than 4, kept 4.
  - **Decided.** Water follows the relief only (not the wave), so lakes are level. The relief is not damped in towns (a gradient scaled round a town centre makes steep rims); `RELIEF` 7 is a look (the largest relief slope is 8%): raise it only with the road and lot slopes in mind.
