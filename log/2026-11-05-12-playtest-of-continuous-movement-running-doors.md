- **2026-11-05, playtest of continuous movement: running, doors, debug arenas (branch continuous-movement, schema 65).** Player: "walking feels better, but running doesn't seem to work. Doors should be something you open and close (or leave open). A debug level in the main menu with a zombie (and a crowd) from the start."
  - Running did switch on (Shift reached the sim), but a run took only a tick off each tile (a third faster) and winded you after about 25 tiles with nothing said. Now: RUN_PCT 175% of walking speed, RUN_TICK_COST 1 endurance a tick (about 10 s from full to winded); the control line says "running" or "WINDED: no running".
  - Doors are open or closed (`grid.open`, closed by default). Use one (E or right-click) to open or close it; not while someone stands in it, and a locked door won't open (locking shuts it). The survivor you control needs a door open (mover 'free'); zombies walk through open doors and not shut ones (crawlers likewise); colonists open and close as they pass, so for them every door is a way through and they leave it as they found it; raiders open unlocked doors. Drawn open as the frame with the leaf swung to one side. Taken over in a closed doorway, you can walk out.
  - Debug arenas (arena.ts), on the New game screen: a 64x64 solo world with one static zombie 5 tiles off, or a crowd of 30 in a ring 8 tiles out; static zombies don't leave at dawn; no neighbours.
