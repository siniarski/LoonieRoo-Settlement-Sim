- **2026-11-12, a second quality pass over the game (`godot/`)**, one of three sessions (the sim and the tests had the others), after some seventy commits from parallel sessions since the first pass (log/2026-11-11-11-quality-pass.md). Nothing a player sees or does was to change; each step is its own commit on a green `./check.sh --all`.
  - **What changed** (ten commits, oldest first).
    - **WorldMesh in parts** (28a44df). The 751-line file was the ground, the banks, the ground edges, the lane lines, road corners, walls, doors, windows and furniture boxes. Now one partial class in four files: `WorldMesh.cs` (a chunk's walk, its signature and lookups, ceilings, furniture boxes, the box and quad), `.Ground.cs` (a tile's ground: `TileGround` chooses; kerbs, lanes, road corners, water, under a bridge), `.Smooth.cs` (the banks and the edges between kinds of ground), `.Walls.cs` (edges and the tile door). The bank and the ground edge share one polygon cut (`Cut`) and one fan (`Fan`); `Verge` and `Shore` (which differed only in how far they looked) are one `Nearest`; a tile's speckle (`Speckle`) and its looks (`GroundLook`, `BedLook`, `SheetLook`) are written once, and the far view and the map pictures take the same speckle. The bank's cut is the edge's with each corner's water less a half: the same crossing points to the bit (both are sixteenths).
    - **FirstPerson's frame as steps** (fd76226): the fields grouped as the frame uses them (the scene; the land, who is about, things, you; what is read from outside), `Update` the steps in order with `PlaceEye` and `Land` pulled out, and a summary of what each step needs from the ones before. The calls, their order, the constructor and the field initialisers' order are unchanged.
    - **One id hash and one node finder** (65e578d): `App/IdHash.cs` (`Fnv`, `Mix`, `Hash01`: Looks, Animals, Furniture and Items each had a copy) and `App/Nodes.cs` (`Find`, `FindAll`: a dozen copies in People, Animals, Vat, OutfitMerge, Wardrobe and the labs), taken with `using static`.
    - **Settings** (330f944): four labs wrote the normal settings out field by field (without the wildfire rate); they use `Game.CopySettings` as the game and the self-test do.
    - **The items and the furniture on ModelSet** (4f8e307): `ModelSet` takes the metal and roughness numbers (the items' are glossier), picks variants (`Pick`), draws a fallback for "" (the items' grey box) and shares one material per shader text; `ItemModels` and `FurnitureModels` keep only where things stand. `ItemModels.MeshNamed` (a second name for `MeshFor`) went.
    - **People in more parts** (e4104ba): `People.Clips.cs` (choosing a body's clip) and `People.You.cs` (your own body), moved unchanged; the header lists all seven parts.
    - **The inventory's controls** (d607a7e): GridView, SlotTile, QuickTile, Meter and NodeAdd moved from `Ui/Inventory.cs` to `Ui/InventoryTiles.cs`.
    - **Read from the sim** (8d3d5e8): `Held.Family` from the weapon table's `cls` and `twoHanded` (every row gives the family the lists gave; an old "gun" is a pistol, as `Weapons.specOf` says); `Fires` reads a scar through `FireMod.scarOf` instead of unpacking its bits.
    - **README and dead names** (07b0195): `godot/README.md` names DeathScreen, SwimHands, People.Hold, People.Swim and the new parts; every flag GameArgs reads is in it. The compiler's three unused names in the game went (`People.leapShots`, AnimTest's `Const`, LookLab's `cpu`).
    - **The vault's hand** (ffebdf0): People.Leap's sill and barricade-top heights come from WorldMesh.Walls (`SILL_H`, `BARRICADE_TOP`), not typed again.
  - **Found clean, or fixed since the first pass.** The HUD's conditions and bars now read `CharacterSheet.build` (Card.cs). No flag is parsed and left out of the README. FarField's own smoothing (a blend of corner colours) is a different thing from the near ground's cut, not a fourth copy.
  - **For the player, ranked** (each would change what is seen, or needs the sim).
    1. **The map's colours are three tables that disagree**: `Ui/Minimap.cs:21` and `Ui/WorldMap.cs:23` have the same keys with different values (the minimap darker: grass 44,52,44 against 52,62,50, a wall 139,145,156 against 150,156,166, a road 80,82,86 against 96,98,102), and `Ui/WorldPreviewView.cs:20` a palette of its own (water 40,78,108 against 36,70,94). Decide whether the minimap is meant to be darker; then one table, with a shade if so.
    2. **A rare water-material slip**: `WorldMesh.Ground.cs` `TileGround`'s last line says "water here" for any tile whose ground is a bridge while roads are ribbons, even one that is not floor (so no water was drawn). If no other tile in the chunk drew water, the empty water surface is committed and `SurfaceSetMaterial(last, Water)` may put the water's material on the ground's surface. Kept exactly as it was; the fix is to return true only for floor.
    3. **The options say "Near only (about 60 m)"** (`Ui/Menu.cs:429`); the code and the README say 56.
    4. **Numbers the game works out again where the sim could say**: a wolf's or bear's last bite from `nextBite - *_BITE_EVERY` (`World3D/Animals.cs:111`), and the side a blow came from, from the shove (`People.Clips.cs` `BlowFrom`). Each wants a field or a function in the sim (a last-bite tick; the blow's direction).
    5. **Per-frame costs** (rule 14, none slow today): `World3D/Roofs.cs:99` sums `state.world.generated` (one entry per chunk: 98,000 on a 10,000 world) every frame to see whether a chunk was made; a count in the sim would do. The sounds still walk every zombie more than once a frame (`Audio/Sounds.cs:130`, `:145`).
    6. **Still left from the first pass**: the far view repeats `Worldgen.drawTerrain` and `drawTrees` (FarField.cs); `Held.cs` sways the off hand over 6 ticks whatever the swing takes; about 500 lab-only lines in `godot/Characters/` (Wardrobe, OutfitMerge, Vat), and two types called `Outfit`.
