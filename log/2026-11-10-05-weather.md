  - **Temperature** (`cs/Sim/Systems/Weather.cs`, derived, never stored): `C + amp * bracket(hour) + (night ? -0.5 : +0.5) + event`. `C` is the temperature at dusk and dawn (21:00 and 05:00, where the sim's night begins and ends), by region and season, pinned to 0 where the old rule flips between day and night (cold region's autumn, any mild winter, a nuclear winter) and well clear of 0 where it never flips. `bracket` is 0 at 21:00 and 05:00, at most 0 at night (trough 03:00), at least 0 by day (peak 14:30), so a weather's amplitude (6, 0.8 to 1.2 by a hash of the day, halved under cloud) never moves the crossing; the 1 degree step at 21:00 and 05:00 makes "below freezing" equal `Climate.coldFor` on every tick. A blizzard is -14, a heatwave up to +6 by day only; rain, snow and storm do not cool the air (that would move the crossing). Scorching (hot south, summer day) is 30 degrees or more. Indoors never under 8. `Climate.regionCold` and `scorching` now call `coldFor` and `scorchingFor`, same results. Test: every region x season x weather x nuclear winter x quarter hour for 21 days, 0 mismatches.
