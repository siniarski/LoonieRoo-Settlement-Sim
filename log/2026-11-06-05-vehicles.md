- **2026-11-06, vehicles (branch vehicles).** System 8. New vehicles.ts; state.vehicles (missing in older saves: vehiclesOf reads it as none).
  - Cars are their own movers, not furniture: a centre in SUB units, a heading (one of 32, with a hard-coded integer cos/sin table so no floats decide anything), a speed, fuel, health and a driver.
  - Getting in and out: use a car next to you (E or right-click). With a fuel can in your bag and room in the tank, that fills it (FUEL_PER_CAN, half a tank); otherwise you get in. Use anything to get out, onto the nearest free tile beside it. Letting go of control, or dying, puts you out too.
  - Controls: W accelerates (ACCEL to MAX_SPEED: about 6 tiles a second against walking's 2), and S brakes, then reverses. Nothing held rolls to a stop. A and D turn a step every TURN_EVERY ticks, only while moving, and the other way in reverse.
  - Collisions: the car is a circle of VEHICLE_RADIUS sliding on walls like any mover (motion.ts slide now takes a radius). It uses a new 'vehicle' mover: floor only, and no border but a bare one, so no doors (even open), windows or barricades. Trees don't block cars yet: they're furniture, not tiles.
  - Fuel burns a point a tick moving, a point every 4 idling. Empty, or wrecked (health 0), it won't go.
  - Noise: the engine is noise 'engine' (18) every 20 ticks while you're in it.
  - Crashes: hitting a wall above CRASH_SPEED stops you dead and dents the car.
  - The dead: running one down at RAM_SPEED or more does 3 a unit of speed (a kill at full speed) and knocks it aside; the car loses a quarter of its speed and a little health. Zombies can't bite you through the car: their bites batter it instead (zombies.ts → batterVehicle).
  - Generated: about 60% of town lots (houses, shops, civic, industry) have a car parked on the near lane of the street in front, two tiles out past the verge, along the street. Each has up to 60% fuel and 40-100% health, by hash. That's about 10 in a 256 world. (The first try parked on the verge, one tile out, at 30% of house and shop lots: 3 or 4 a world, on grass.)
  - The expedition truck (outposts.ts) is still furniture, apart from this.
  - Debug: a spawnVehicle command. Drawn as a painted body turned to its heading with a windscreen; wrecks are grey. The HUD shows speed, fuel and the car's condition while you drive.
  - tests/vehicles.test.ts (8). Checked in the browser: got in, turned, reached top speed, HUD readout. Colony harness: days 20, 25 (alive at the end), 21. Solo: days 3, 4, 3 (the cars shift ids, so the world's rolls differ). Bench unchanged. 733 tests.
