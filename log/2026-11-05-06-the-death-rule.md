- **2026-11-05, the death rule (succession.ts, schema 60):** when the survivor you control dies, `state.succession` records who and where, and a panel asks (pausing the game the first time): take over someone in your group with resolve 40+ (no children; most resolved first), start anew (a new survivor 60-90 tiles off in the same world, alone, with a crude weapon and a bandage, a little ground around them seen), or just watch the colony. Followers follow whoever you take over. Anyone else dying asks nothing.
