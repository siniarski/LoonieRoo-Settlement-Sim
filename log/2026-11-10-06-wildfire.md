- **2026-11-10, wildfire: fire spreads through the living world and is drawn** (the player asked to "introduce fire spreading"; `FireMod.cs`, `godot/World3D/Fires.cs`). Fire already burned buildings; now it runs through grass, forest undergrowth and trees, across yards and through woods, stops at roads, bare ground, water and rain, leaves scars, and shows in first person (flames, smoke, embers, scorched ground, charred stumps).
  - **What burns** (`FireMod.fuelAt`, read from the tile each time, never stored): Structure (wall, door, barricade, furniture: the old rules, unchanged), Tree (furniture `tree`), Undergrowth (ground `forest` with no tree: the game's ferns and bushes), Grass (ground `grass` or `field`). Never: roads, paving, dirt, bridges, concrete, indoor floors (`Rooms.isIndoors`), farm plots, water, reinforced walls, a tile whose scar hasn't regrown, ungenerated chunks. The sim has no bush or stump entity: bushes are the forest ground's undergrowth, and a burnt tree is simply gone (as burnt furniture always was) with a scar of kind tree, which the game draws as a charred stump.
  - **Constants as built, and why.** Burn ticks: grass 40 (four spread checks: the first cut had 60 and percolated across whole worlds), undergrowth 90, tree 300 (= FIRE_BURN), bare ground 100 (old). Vegetation looks for neighbours every 10 ticks per tile on its own beat (buildings keep the shared 20-tick beat). Catch per check on a day of dryness 1 (a summer heatwave): grass 0.5, undergrowth 0.3, tree 0.15 times dryness squared (green wood needs dry weather twice over), structure from a vegetation fire 0.02 (a house in a burning yard catches in a heatwave, rarely otherwise); times the burning tile's heat (grass 0.8, undergrowth 0.9, tree and structure 1); capped 0.95; times `settings.wildfire`. Building to building: the old rule exactly (0.2, doubled in a heatwave, the old 3-number hash). Dryness = season (summer 0.4, autumn 0.3, spring 0.1, winter 0.05) x weather (clear 1, heatwave 2.2, storm 0.15, rain 0.1, snow 0.02, blizzard 0) x hour (night down to 0.4). Why these: with four checks a tile, a fire passes a tile with probability 1-(1-p)^4, and the square lattice percolates at about one half; a clear summer day is p = 0.16 (the edge: most fires die small, a few run), a heatwave 0.35 (runs far), autumn 0.12 and spring 0.04 (die out). Rain burns vegetation fires down six times faster (a grass fire is out in about 10 ticks; buildings keep the old 2x). Damage standing in a grass or undergrowth fire 3 a tick (a flash: crossing one costs 10 to 20 of 1000), buildings and trees the old 5. Things lying on burning grass survive it; on a building's floor or bare ground they are ash, as before.
  - **Bounds.** MAX_FIRES 150 -> 1500 (a 38 x 38 stand; every cost below is bounded by budgets, not by it). At most SPREAD_BUDGET = 150 spread checks a tick however many fires burn, starting at a different place each tick when there are more (a skipped tile is checked at its next turn: a big fire spreads a little slower, never costs more). Fires by tile are indexed outside state (`FireIndex`, rebuilt if the list changes behind it); the survivors/zombies-in-fire scan is one lookup each, not fires x people. Fires far from everyone (no survivor within one 32-tile chunk of them, NEAR_CHUNKS = 1, always generated) don't spread, hurt or get stepped each tick: they cost a set lookup a tick and are aged in batches of FAR_EVERY = 100 ticks until they burn out where they stand (a wildfire can't run unseen across a region). Scars (`state.burnt`) are capped at 40,000 (grass stops catching there) and forgotten after SCAR_DAYS = 10 (half a year: the next spring's green-up). Survivors fight a vegetation fire only within 12 tiles of them and 4 of a building, at most 12 such jobs (`worthFighting`, checked every tenth tick when there are over 16 fires): they defend the house, they don't walk into a burning wood. Buildings, bare-ground fires and (wildfire 0) trees get jobs as before.
  - **Causes.** The existing ones, spreading outward (stove, firebomb, heater, lightning, the Burn order), and a new one: a fuelled brazier (`heater` with fuel) standing out of doors throws a spark on a neighbouring tile that can burn, 0.005 an in-game hour times dryness (about one day in ten in a heatwave, one in twenty on a clear summer day). There is no campfire in the sim; the brazier is the nearest "fire left burning". Not added (options for the player): lightning in dry thunderstorms (the existing storm lightning is wet: dryness 0.15), arson by raiders beyond the firebomb, a lit campfire or torch the player can drop, a firebreak tool (mow or dig a strip), wind (a direction the front leans), embers jumping a road, fire spreading to farm crops, wood piles and stockpiles burning on grass, survivors fleeing a front (pathfinding ignores fire).
  - **World setting.** `settings.wildfire` (default 1; 0 = vegetation never burns and trees follow the old rule; 2 = twice the chances). Default 1 is what the balance runs below used. Older saves read 1.
