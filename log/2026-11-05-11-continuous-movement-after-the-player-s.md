- **2026-11-05, continuous movement (branch continuous-movement), after the player's first playtest note: "not a fan of the fixed tile positioning for manual control".** Decided: the world stays on the grid; movers get precise positions; everything migrates, on a branch.
  - A: motion.ts. Every mover (survivor, zombie, dog, deer, resident) has `at` and `was` in fixed-point SUB units (256 a tile; integers, so replays and a port stay exact; sqrt not hypot). `pos` stays the tile under `at`. Tile steps set `at` as they go; the renderer blends `was` to `at`. Schema 64.
  - B: you move freely: the held direction moves your centre each tick at your tile pace (64 units a tick at 4 ticks a tile), the same speed any way you go, sliding along walls as a circle of radius 77 (0.3 of a tile). Running costs endurance per tile's worth of ground. Letting go stops you where you are.
  - C: zombies own their position: they glide along their tile paths (speed rounded up, so each tile still takes exactly its ticks: old timings and tests hold); within 2.5 tiles of their target with a clear line they come straight at where you stand, stopping just inside reach (282: orthogonal neighbours yes, diagonal no); they push apart in crowds (bucketed by tile, pairs in id order) and out of people, dogs, deer and residents; they attack anyone within reach. A step that gets walled off mid-glide turns back. Your swings hit the nearest zombie (or resident, or deer) in a 120 degree arc within 1.25 tiles (integer test: dot > 0, 4 dot^2 >= |f|^2 |d|^2). Guards swing by the same reach and stop walking to fight (interruptForFight): before that, a guard walked through a homing zombie to the tile it had been on, and took 223 damage instead of under 200 (the one-on-one balance test caught it). Dogs bite, and residents hit, by reach.
  - D: what's left on tiles: AI survivors, dogs, deer and residents still walk tile paths (drawn from precise positions). Corner-cutting (string-pulled paths) would change every job's timing and most tests for little gain: a later option.
  - Costs: about 200 zombies, 0.37 ms a tick mean, 7.8 ms worst (bench). 639 tests.
