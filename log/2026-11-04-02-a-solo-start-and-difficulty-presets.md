- **2026-11-04, a solo start and difficulty presets:** `state.settings` (difficulty.ts), schema 50, chosen in the Game menu when making a new world. Presets: Settler (a group, 60% zombies, mild weather, weeks since the outbreak), Survivor (the usual game, exactly as before), Apocalypse (alone, 150%, harsh, a year on), Nuclear winter (alone, 125%, extreme, years on, winter). The sandbox row changes any setting after picking a preset.
  - Start: a group of three, or one person alone in the hut with one bed, 4 wood and 4 food, under your control from the first tick.
  - Zombies: a percentage of each night's count and of those asleep in ruins.
  - Weather (mild, normal, harsh, extreme): weather events 0.5x-2x as often; cold damage every 20, 10, 7 or 5 ticks.
  - Progression (weeks, months, a year, years): crate loot 1.5x, 1x, 0.6x, 0.35x; scavenged weapons and clothing come 0%, 0%, 30%, 50% worn; 0, 0, 10%, 20% more ruin walls fallen in.
  - Nuclear winter: crops grow at half speed; every night is cold outdoors, not just winter's.
  Not yet: radioactive areas, regional climates (hot or cold regions), map types (island, island chain, mainland). Normal settings make the identical world (a test checks).
