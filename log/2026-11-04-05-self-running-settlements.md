- **2026-11-04, self-running settlements:** neighbours are now people (5-12 to start), food (in days of eating, starting at 3 a head) and walls (0-5), changed every dawn from a hash of their id and the day (colonies.ts, schema 53). They farm by season (1.25, 1.4, 1.0, 0.4 a head; halved in a nuclear winter), eat one a head, and lose 4% of stores a day. With nothing left about one in seven starve (and a starving one thinks a little less of you each day). Well fed, now and then someone joins them and the walls go up. Night attacks take one or two (likelier later, and with more zombies; good walls turn most away). A fed one sends food to a starving neighbour; otherwise the starving may raid the nearest one with food. Past 16 people a few split off to found a new place (up to 6 settlements). At none left it falls, and its compound is a ruin with the dead asleep inside.
  When anyone of yours comes within 24 tiles, it unfolds into a compound: walls and a door (broken through, if it fell), a bed a head, tables, and its people standing about (drawn like wanderers with their settlement's initial; not yet simulated one by one). Their food stays a number, so your haulers don't walk off with it.
  You and them: food sent to a starving place counts double and feeds them. Raid (Neighbours panel) sends everyone armed, settled from the numbers (you: 2 a weapon, +2 a gun, times 0.5-1.5; them: people + 2 a wall): win and half their food comes home (up to 10), lose and one of yours comes back badly hurt; either way they count you an enemy. Friendly ones sending you someone now lose that person.
  Tuning: a 45-day run of three left alone: winters are tight, there are a few raids between them, they decline slowly, and one in three or so falls by day 45. First pass (more food, no spoilage) never starved anyone; a second (less food, 5% spoilage, attacks rising faster) wiped everyone out in the first winter.
  Not yet: residents as real people (walking, talking, trading in person, fighting back); their own expeditions and caravans; their attitude to each other driving alliances; recruiting from them in person.
