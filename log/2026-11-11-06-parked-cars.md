- **2026-11-11, parked cars are in the way.** After the car's body became car-sized (log 04), people, the dead and animals still walked through a parked one. The player asked for parked cars to be solid to them too.
  - **Decided** (`cs/Sim/Movement/VehicleParking.cs`, part of `Vehicles`).
    - **By tile, through pathfinding.** Movers on foot never asked about cars: free movers (you, the dead, animals, dogs) are stopped by `Motion.fits`, which asks `Pathfinding.isPassable` of every tile their body overlaps, and tile walkers (colonists, residents) follow A* paths made of the same answer. So a parked car marks the tiles whose centre is inside its body (five for a sedan along a row; a stair for one on the slant) in a new layer of the grid, `parkedLayer`, and `isPassable` is false there for every mover but a car (cars meet body to body). Paths go round, the dead stop at it, reachability knows (the change is noted like a wall's, and a chunk with a mark is no longer "bare").
    - **Not saved.** The layer is exactly what `state.vehicles` says, so it is `[JsonIgnore]` and laid in again when a state is read (`SimState.OnDeserialized` calls `Vehicles.remask`): no schema change, and a loaded game and one that ran on agree (a test plays the same commands on a state and its JSON copy and compares the two).
    - **Kept in step** at every place a car's standing changes: `addVehicle` (park), `enterVehicle` (unpark), `leaveVehicle` and a driver who is gone (park), the expedition truck leaving (unpark), arriving at an outpost, stranded and coming home (park), a chunk's cars taken into the world (`mark`). `park` and `unpark` bump `walkabilityVersion`. `unpark` leaves a tile another parked car is over. `Vehicles.remove` is for scenes and tests.
    - **Who was there.** When a car comes to rest, a tile walker whose tile is under it and a free mover whose body overlaps a tile under it are put on the nearest open tile (`Motion.place`): a body in something solid can't take a step. Getting out, the car is marked first and you are then placed at the door if a body fits there, else on the nearest clear tile.
    - **You stop at the body.** The body (1.7 m) is wider than the row under it (1 m), and from first person a third of a metre inside a car's flank shows, so your walking is also pushed off the body itself (`pushOut`: a strip with round ends). Only you: the dead and animals stop at the tiles, because their paths are tiles and what stops them must agree with what they planned.
  - **Checked.** Do generated cars cut places off? For six 256 worlds (77 cars) every walkable tile round each car can still reach every other: none does. Tick cost (bench, one run each way): no difference outside the run-to-run noise (0.09-1.4 ms a tick either way). An existing pose test steered you through where the car now stands and was moved into the open.
