- **2026-11-11, animals at parked cars.** Since log 06 a parked car stopped every animal and dog: a deer could be cornered against one, a dog left behind by one. The player asked for some animals to go under cars and the rest over them. Now the small go under and the rest go over, by kind and by car (`cs/Sim/World/AnimalCars.cs`).
  - **The table** (ticks for a car's width, two tiles; a longer crossing takes longer in proportion). Heights from the models (art/scripts/build_furniture.py; animals from their .json):

    | kind | sedan (under 0.28 m, roof 1.28) | pickup (under 0.45 m, bed 1.0, cab 1.75) |
    |---|---|---|
    | rabbit (0.35 m) | under, 8 | under, 7 |
    | dog (0.67 m) | over, 9 | under, 14 (a belly-crawl) |
    | deer (1.43 m) | leap, 6 | leap, 7 |
    | wolf (1.0 m) | over, 10 | over, 13 |
    | bear (1.0 m) | over, 30 | over, 40 |

  - **How.** A crossing is a short straight move it can't steer out of, as your leaps are: `cross` on the animal or dog (how, from, to, ticks, done, the car's height there, ticks hidden), each tick a share of what's left in integers, so it is saved and replays. It does nothing else while crossing (no bite, no turn). Two ways in: A* may cross a car's tiles at a cost by kind (a new optional `overCar` cost in `Pathfinding.findPathToAny`: rabbit 1, deer 2, dog and wolf 3, bear 8 tiles' worth), and `Walkers.followPath` starts a crossing when the next waypoint is under a car; or a flee leg picked straight across a car (a rabbit or a deer at once, the rest only if nothing else is open: `Walkers.fleeLeg`), walked up to the car and crossed where it meets it (`AnimalCars.straightOver`). The far side is checked before (the body fits: no wall, car, rim or trunk; the line is open but for cars: `AnimalCars.lineOver`, walkLine's rule with car tiles open) and every tick (a wall built or a car parked there: it stops and is put on the nearest tile it can stand). One that can't cross is refused and plans off cars for 60 ticks, so it goes round or stands; it never presses against the car. `Pathfinding.isPassable` is unchanged: a body never stands where it doesn't fit except while crossing, so collision and planning agree, and nothing caches reachability for animals (their A* is bounded and local).
  - **Under** is out of reach (`AnimalCars.hidden`): a shot passes it by (Shooting), a colonist hunting it waits (Work.workHunt), a predator doesn't pick it as prey (so a wolf loses the rabbit it was after), a zombie's blow on a dog under the pickup lands on the car (Dogs.hitDog). A rabbit stays under, halfway, while something it fears is within FLEE (HOLD, 150 ticks at most). **Over** is in plain sight and can be hit, but can't bite.
  - **A walker fix found on the way.** Walking straight at a goal, a body caught on a corner (a car's end here; a wall's too) slid sideways into it and the path pulled it back, for ever: the line was open, the body is wider than it. `Walkers.walkTo` now takes the straight step only if it gets at least a quarter of a step closer, else follows the path round. It inches round the corner (a few ticks slow), but gets on. No other test moved.
  - **Drawing.** `AnimalCars.lift` (metres, like `Leaps.lift`): a leap is an arc 0.3 m over the car where it crosses, a climb goes up onto the car (`topAt`: the sedan's roof or bonnet, the pickup's cab, bonnet or bed) and down; `AnimalCars.room` is the room under the car, and godot/World3D/Animals.cs crouches an animal under one to fit (a squash in height) and faces a crossing animal the way it crosses.
