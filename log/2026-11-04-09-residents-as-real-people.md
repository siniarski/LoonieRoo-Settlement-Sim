- **2026-11-04, residents as real people:** an unfolded settlement's people are `state.residents` (residents.ts, schema 55), one a head, synced at unfolding and every dawn (newcomers at the door; the dead and departed vanish). They walk about inside their walls. An enemy settlement's people come out at your people within 8 tiles and hit for 25 (armour helps). Hitting one of a peaceful settlement's people makes it an enemy on the spot; a dead resident is one fewer at home, and leaves a named body. In person (T near them, or right-click one): hand over what you carry for food from their stores (medicine 3, gun 4, armour 3, ammo/weapon/coat 2, most else 1), ask one to come with you if they're friendly (40+) and have more than 3, or just talk (+2, once a day). Drawn like wanderers with their settlement's initial; an enemy's in red.
