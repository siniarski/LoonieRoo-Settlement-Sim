    1. **The storyteller lost deaths.** `Director.recentDeaths` searched the event log for " died of ", but the log keeps the last 50 events: in a busy colony a death hours old fell out of the count (and any message with those words counted). Deaths go to `state.deathTicks` (pruned to the window) from `Needs.killSurvivor`. Old saves load with none: the storyteller forgets the day and a half before the load. Test: "the storyteller > counts a death after the event log has moved on".
    2. **A trap's or a dog's kill never left a cure sample** (a guard's and a turret's did). `Zombies.killZombie` (gone, a body, maybe a sample) is the one death for turret, trap and dog. Test: "spike traps > a zombie a trap kills may leave a sample".
    3. **A hostile resident's blow was health and nothing else**, and reached you in a car or under one. `Residents.hitSurvivor`: the car checks, a wound chance, "attacked", "shaken", panic and a startle, as a wolf's bite and a zombie's. Test: "residents > a hostile one's blow frightens and shakes you".
    4. **Rationing's text said a quarter** where 3 ticks in 4 makes food last a third longer. Test: in "policies > rationing makes food last longer".
    5. **A hurt worker with no remedy about searched every item for one each tick** (`Needs.interruptForNeeds`). `remedyToGoFor` waits TREAT_RETRY_TICKS, as `startTreatment` and thirst do. Test: "health and death > a hurt worker with no bandage about asks again later".
    6. **Your heir kept their job** (and bed, meal, book): `SuccessionMod.take` skipped `Commands.dropEverything`, which takeControl does. Test: "the death rule > the heir puts down their job".
    7. **`JsRecord` put a new key in a removed one's slot** (a .NET Dictionary does), so it enumerated mid-list, not last as in JS, and a save taken while the hole was there played on in another order (opinions after a death, fire scars). Test: "records keep JS order > a key added after a removal comes last".
    8. **`Arena.openNear`** returned the top-left open tile of the first square with one, not the nearest. Test: "the arena > openNear gives the nearest open tile of the ring".
    9. **`JobChoice.chooseJob` ("pure") moved a scout job** to its next unseen tile while only asked. The plan carries `moveTo`; `claim` moves it. Test: "exploring > asking which job a scout would take moves no job".
    10. **The water chilled you twice as fast at a 2x day** (`Swim.soak`, on slow ticks, took `tick % N`, the trap 2026-11-12-06 took out of BodyMod). Counts slow ticks. Test: "swim > the water chills you as fast a day at a longer day".

Out from under and onto the nearest ground a body fits. Test: "animals and cars > letting go under a pickup leaves the colonist beside it".
