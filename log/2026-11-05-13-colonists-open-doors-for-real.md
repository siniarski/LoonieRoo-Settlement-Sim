- **2026-11-05, colonists open doors for real (doors.ts, schema 66).** Player: "colonists just walk through closed doors (they need to be able to open and close behind them)". Before, colonists treated every door as a way through without touching it. Now a colonist (with keys to locked doors) or a raider (unlocked only) opens a closed door as they step into the doorway; it's listed in `grid.passing` and swings shut once nobody is in it or stepping into it. A door you opened by hand isn't "passing", so it stays open; using a door that's held open for someone makes it yours (it stays open). While a door is held open a zombie can slip through. None of this bumps walkabilityVersion (survivors path through doors either way; zombies check each next tile), so passing a door costs no repathing or room recomputing.
