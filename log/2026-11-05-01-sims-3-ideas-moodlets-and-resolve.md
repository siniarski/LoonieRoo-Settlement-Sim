- **2026-11-05, Sims 3 ideas: moodlets and Resolve.** Direction agreed: moodlets that do things, a few higher needs above survival, and Sims-style wishes, guarded against bloat. The "hope / commitment" number is called **Resolve** (the player picked it over Trust, Purpose, Belonging). Built so far:
  - Moodlets (moodlets.ts): some thoughts now do something while they last. Well rested (woke rested in a bed, 6h): works a bit faster, swings 2 ticks sooner. Blood up (a kill, 1h): hits 20% harder. Shaken (got hit, 1h): swings 3 ticks later. Out in the cold: a tick slower a tile. Shown in the mood tooltip and, for you, in the control line.
  - Resolve (resolve.ts, `survivor.resolve`, 0-100, schema 56): starts 40-60 (a hash of the id). Each dawn it drifts with mood (about 1 a day per 8 mood above or below 50, at most 4) and anyone alone loses 2 more. A request seen through +10 (a relative found on the radio +15), a confession +5, a silent radio -10, a friend's death -10, anyone's death -3. Under 20 (breaking): may walk away in the night (never the one you control; no body, no mourning); under your control, a quarter of swings freeze. 80 or more (steeled): no breakdowns. Resolve column in the survivor table; yours in the control line.
  Next, as agreed: Social and Comfort as higher needs feeding mood (and so resolve), then wishes and promises (small generated wants, one or two each, from systems we already have; a promised wish pays more, a broken one costs resolve). Resolve is also the number for the death rule (who will take over).
