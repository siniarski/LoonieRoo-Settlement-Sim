- **2026-11-11, over and onto parked cars.** The player: "Can we have zombies that vault or crawl over obstacles they
  typically couldn't make it over? Same thing with jumping." This is the jumping half (the dead and the animals were
  parallel sessions): Space now gets you over a parked car or onto it, and you can walk on its roof and get down.
  The rules are in `cs/Sim/Movement/CarLeaps.cs` (part of Leaps); `ControlMod` calls it in two places.
  - **What Space does now.** Within CAR_REACH (320 SUB, a swing's reach) of a parked car ahead, on the nearest of the
    four sides of your way:
    - walking into it, **over** it when a body fits past the far side: a slide over the bonnet or the boot (8 ticks,
      10 endurance), over a pickup's bed behind its cab (10, 12), a scramble over the roof (12, 14: anything that
      crosses the cabin, side to side or nose to tail); no faster than 64 SUB a tick, so nose to tail on a sedan is
      23 ticks. A vault is 4 ticks and 6.
    - standing still facing it, or walking into one with no room past it, **onto** it (8 ticks, 10), landing 96 SUB
      inside its body.
    - **on top** (`control.onCar`): walking within the body (your centre in it, no wall, door or window within your
      body); off an edge, a step down (4 ticks, nothing spent) where a body fits below, else you stop at the edge and
      slide along it; Space jumps down on your way (6 ticks, the jump's 4), or where you stand if there is no room.
    - winded, none of them (the step down by walking still works).
    - The landing is fixed and checked at take-off: the first spot past the car (CLEAR beyond it, then 16 SUB at a
      time for 128) where a body fits on the ground, clear of every parked car and trunk, with no edge between the car
      and it. `busy.targetId` is `x,y,car,fromCm,toCm,peakCm`; old leaps keep `x,y`.
  - **Kept right.** `Leaps.settle` at the start of each controlled tick: the car gone (Vehicles.remove), driven off
    (unparked, a driver), got into from its roof (enterVehicle), or you off its body, and `onCar` is cleared and you
    are put where a body fits (where you are, else the nearest tile clear of everything). A leap in the air is left to
    its landing, which checks again. Letting go of control, or taking another survivor, does the same (`Leaps.letGo`).
    A save carries `onCar` and your position: it loads on the roof and plays on the same (tested, as is a save in the
    air). No schema bump: `onCar` is null on the ground and left out of the save then.
  - **The view.** `Leaps.height(state, s, alpha)` is your feet over the ground: an old leap's arc as before; a car
    leap's from its take-off height to its landing's through a peak, never inside the car it is over; on top, the
    car's top under you (a profile along its length from the models: the sedan's boot and bonnet 0.78 m, roof 1.28 m;
    the pickup's bed 1 m, cab roof 1.75 m, bonnet 1.25 m, ramps for the glass). `FirstPerson.Eye`, your body
    (People.Leap.cs) and `PoseIntents.lift` read it. Your body's foot IK took the terrain as its ground and pulled the
    lifted body back down into the car (head and shoulders through the roof): your own feet's ground is now the car's
    top where you stand on one (`People.OwnGroundAt`). "Over" plays the vault clip; climb and down the jump clips.
  - **A plain jump into a car** stopped at its tiles but not at its body (jumps skipped `pushOut`): a jump on a slant
    into a sedan's flank ended with your centre 3 SUB inside it. The jump's tick now pushes you out as walking does.
  - **The dead on a roof (to settle at the merge).** They reach you by distance as ever (Motion.REACH 282, centre to
    centre, height not counted): beside a sedan (a tile from its middle line, 256 SUB) they reach you on its roof. A
    roof is a lookout, not a refuge. The dead's session makes them climb parked cars; with this rule one on the roof
    with you changes nothing, but whoever merges should decide whether height should count.
  - **Other low things (task 4).** Looked for what stops you on foot that a person could get over: **nothing else**.
    Furniture doesn't block you on foot at all (counters, tables, beds: you walk through them; only cars treat it as
    solid), so there is nothing to vault there; a barricade *tile* is already walkable for you; barricade edges and
    broken windows are vaulted, a tile of water hopped. Left as they are: glass windows (break them first), boarded
    windows and shut doors, walls, tree trunks, two tiles of water, and the quarantine fence (wall tiles round the
    map's edge: climbing it would undo the zone). Furniture being solid on foot, and then vaultable, is a choice for
    the player.
