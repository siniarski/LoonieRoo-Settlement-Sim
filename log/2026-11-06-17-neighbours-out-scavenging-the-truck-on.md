- **2026-11-06, neighbours out scavenging, the truck on the road, and wildlife (branch wild-roads).** Player: "4" (neighbours' own scavenging runs; the truck seen on the road) "and lets look at adding wildlife that roam. Perhaps a food chain, rabbits and wolfs (who attack survivors if they are hungry or too close). And perhaps the rare bear".
  - **Scavenging runs (runs.ts, state.runs).**
    - At dawn a settlement short of food (40%; well fed, 10%) with 5 or more people may send 3 to a ruin 25-60 tiles off. A marker like a caravan: out a tile every 6 ticks, searching 2 hours, back. Home with 2 food a head.
    - Each hour a chance the dead catch them (2% on the road, 8% at the ruin, rising with the days and the zombie setting): one lost, a body where they fell. Lose them all and their food lies with the bodies, marked on your map "dead scavengers".
    - One of yours within 3 tiles meets them: +3 reputation, and their ruin goes on your map.
    - Drawn in the world, on the minimap and on the map. Run ids are their own (day and settlement), not from nextId.
  - **The truck on the road (expeditions.ts).** When the truck is a vehicle, an expedition drives it out in a straight line, waits while they search, and drives back; it's locked meanwhile (driverId `x:<expedition>`, which vehicles.ts leaves alone). Each hour on the road a 3% chance (scaled by the zombie setting) that the dead catch it: someone's hurt, or killed if hurt already; lose everyone and the truck stands empty where it stopped. An outpost's truck parks at the outpost. Home, it parks by the colony.
  - **Wildlife (animals.ts).**
    - Rabbits: up to 4 counted within 40 tiles of each of yours (2 in winter), turning up 18-34 tiles off and at least 14 from everyone; they bolt from people, predators and the dead within 4, and breed (two close together, 15% an hour, up to 8 about; not in winter).
    - Wolves: a pack of 3, 1% an hour (doubled at night and in winter) with none about; they come in hungry and move on after a day. Hungry, they run down rabbits and deer (a sprint, 2 ticks a tile, quicker than a rabbit). They keep 6 tiles from people unless starving (2 days without food; winter packs arrive nearer it), when they pick someone and come for them. Anyone within 2 gets a warning bite (30), then the wolf keeps clear for 80 ticks. Hurt one and the whole pack is out for you. Badly hurt, they run.
    - Bears: 8% a day, one at a time; slow, 700 health. Within 3 tiles: a maul (90), then it backs off; hurt it and it comes for you.
    - An animal that attacks someone is marked for hunting. Colonists' hunt jobs fight wolves and bears blow by blow (they bite back); rabbits and deer are taken as before. You hit them with hitAnimal (was hitDeer). Meat: rabbit 1, deer 3, wolf 2, bear 6.
    - Only on maps 64 or more across, and gone when nobody of yours is within 70 tiles, so they cost nothing far off. Rolls are hashes, and they number their own ids (state.wildId), so their coming and going shifts nothing else: solo dies on the same days as main.
    - An animal left on a tile that's built over (or newly generated as a wall) steps to open ground.
