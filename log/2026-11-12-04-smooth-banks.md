- **2026-11-12, the water's edge drawn smooth.** After the road ribbons (log 03) the player asked for the same for sidewalks and rivers.
  - **Rivers and lakes** (`WorldMesh.Banks`; nothing in the sim changed). A river is not a line the renderer can follow as a road is: its tiles are stamped from curves with each tile's test point jittered up to 2.5 tiles, so the banks are ragged on purpose and a ribbon along the curve would show grass where you wade. Instead the bank is drawn from the tiles themselves, smoothed: each tile corner is given the share of the sixteen tiles about it that are water, and the bank is the line where that is a half. A tile the line crosses is drawn in two parts (land in its own ground, or the shore's for a water tile; river bed and the water's sheet on the other side), cut along the line at the ground's heights; a tile wholly across the line is drawn as that side. Straight banks stay where they were; steps become slants and curves. Under a bridge the water counts as water, and the bridge's own tiles are left as they are.
  - **Sidewalks**: nothing to do. Town streets are straight rectangles in the plan (`Street`: east-west or north-south), so their sidewalks and kerbs are already straight lines; country roads, which curve, have no sidewalks.
