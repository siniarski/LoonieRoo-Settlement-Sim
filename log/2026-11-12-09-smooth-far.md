- **2026-11-12, the smooth look carried into the distance; the ribbons' joins.** After the road ribbons, smooth banks and smooth ground (logs 03-05) the far view still drew roads, water and ground by tile, a neighbouring region's roads were missing near a region's edge, and the ribbons' joins were ragged. Drawn only: nothing in the sim changed.
  - **The far view** (`FarField.cs`). Each STEP (4 m) corner of the far ground is the mean of the sixteen tiles about it (as the near edges count them), so kinds of ground fade along the lines the near view draws, not in 4 m squares; a corner is water if half of them are, and a triangle with water and land corners is cut where that share is a half (`Banked`): banks as smooth as the near ones, four times coarser. Country roads are the near ribbons, coarser (`Roads`): the plan's lines, a strip a piece every 4 m on the far ground's own triangles, a disc at a junction, a plank-coloured deck over water; their road tiles count as the ground under them. Their lane lines are a second mesh per region seen to 140 m (`LINES_TO`), past which a 12 cm line is under a pixel or two. The far water is the near sheet's colour mixed over the bed's, so the two meet with less of a line. With the far field on, the near ribbons are drawn only inside the near chunks (the hole, a metre over) and the far field draws the rest, so they never lie on each other's ground.
  - **Neighbouring regions** (`RoadNet` in `RoadRibbons.cs`). The roads of every planned region in reach, each line once (a road crossing into the next region is in both plans); a region not planned yet is asked for and the ribbons made again when it is. A straight road can be two points far apart, so "is it in reach" tests its pieces, not its points. Far regions share one net per plan region.
  - **Joins.** A junction (a line's end on or beside another line) gets a disc of asphalt, which fills the corner between the strips, and no edge or centre line is drawn through another road's asphalt or within a road's half-width of a junction. A road that meets a town's street runs on to the street's kerb line (the tile edge where the street's sunk asphalt starts) and is cut there. Over a bridge the ribbon goes on as a deck of planks at the bridge's height, flat across, its sides down 0.55 m; the bridge's tiles are drawn as water under it (`WorldMesh.UnderBridge`), and a river-bed corner out in the water by a bridge is put under the surface (`WorldMesh.Cor`: the bridge's corners stand at deck height and the bed rose to them in spikes).
  - **The half-tile** (harness `road edges`, `cs/Harness/RoadEdges.cs`; docs/roads-off-grid.md has the table). Seeds 1, 2, 3, 42 at 512: 0.03-0.05 m2 a metre of road looks like road and drives as grass, 0.14-0.23 m2 the reverse; 11-14% of road tiles have some; the deepest 0.52 m, the median 0.13 m. 3-4% of the road's area, in a band at its edges: not worth step 2.
