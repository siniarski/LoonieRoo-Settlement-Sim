  - **Decided at the seams.**
    - **Keys.** Lighting and weapons both took X. X reloads (weapons), **Z is the light switch**, G the flashlight or headlights, Q the next weapon. The HUD's key line, docs/controls.md and the self-test say so.
    - **Flags.** Growth and weather each added `--day` with a different meaning: there is one now, weather's (`--day=N` is 06:00 of day N, a fraction counts from 06:00), and the world is grown as of that day (`Growth.update`); `--hour=H` (lighting) picks the hour. Wildfire's `--fire-weather` is `--weather`; its `--fire=dx,dy,..` is `--burn=` because lighting's `--fire=X,Y` (one fire at a tile) had the name. `Game.StartDay` is gone.
    - **Fire and growth.** A tile of grass, undergrowth or a tree that burns out has its growth started again (`Growth.clear` in `FireMod.consume`: the hook both sessions left for each other).
    - **Fire and snow.** Grass and undergrowth under lying snow don't catch (`Snow.depthAt` in `FireMod.spread`); a tree does.
    - **Weapons, the flashlight and the grid inventory.** `Inventory.ITEM_SIZE` has a footprint for each new kind (knife 1x2; hatchet, machete, crowbar, pipe 1x3; bat, plank 1x4; spear 1x5; sledge 2x4; pistol, revolver 2x2; shotgun 2x4; rifle 2x5; crossbow 3x3; ammunition boxes 1x1; the flashlight 1x1): 12 kg of any one kind still fits the 8x6 bag, which the grid's own test demands. Quick slots take any wieldable weapon and the flashlight (`Inventory.isQuickKind`); the Tab screen's menu says "Switch" for a flashlight.
    - **The sky.** FirstPerson darkened the sky on any weather but clear; Weather.cs's overcast does that now, so the old line went (a heatwave's sky was grey).
