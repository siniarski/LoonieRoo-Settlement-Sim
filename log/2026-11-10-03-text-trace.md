- **2026-11-10, text instead of pictures.** The player asked why agent sessions are slow; one cause is the loop of screenshots to learn where the body was (a launch of ~3.5 s and a ~450 KB picture to read each, in a session about walking into trees where a few lines of text would have answered). Added to the game's command line (godot/GameText.cs, Game.cs; godot/README.md has the syntax and a "Which to use when" section at its top):
  - **`--trace[=K] --trace-near=R`:** one line a tick (every Kth; first and last always) for the survivor you control: tick, position in tiles to two decimals, tile, facing in degrees, stance and speed (from `PoseIntents`), health, run/airborne/steer/action; with `--trace-near`, a line for each zombie, animal, dog, survivor or resident within R tiles. With `--trace` or `--textmap` the `--ticks` run one tick at a time in `_Ready` (not in frames), `--at`, `--animals` and `--hold` take effect first, and the game quits after printing unless `--shot` is given. **It works under `--headless`:** no window, no frames, 12 ticks in ~1.5 s (a launch with a window ~3 s). `--hold` keys are applied as commands each tick, as a frame's input would be (a jump again on each landing).
  - **`--textmap=R --textmap-at=T,..`:** `DebugText.dumpText` cropped to the R-tile square round you at the end (and at those ticks), you as `@`. Where edge walls stand in the square it prints the borders-between-tiles map instead (the tile map has no edge walls in it).
  - **`--shot-at=5,10,20` (with `--shot=out.png`):** one picture a tick, `out-5.png`...: the sim only moves to each tick (a tick at a time, so exact), 3 frames to catch up, drawn at alpha 1 (the tick, not a blend). The trace and the maps keep going through those ticks. **`--shot-width=W` / `--shot-scale=F`** save smaller (Lanczos); each shot prints its size and bytes. 1280x720 is 433 KB; 640 wide is a quarter of the pixels (what the model pays for follows pixels, not bytes: the 3D scene's noise keeps PNG bytes high, 512x288 is still ~156 KB).

A `--shot` under `--headless` says it draws nothing and fails (the dummy renderer has no picture). `--shot-at` ticks are absolute sim ticks.
