- **2026-11-08, the tree benchmark.** Player: "did you performance test with lots of trees? lets benchmark and see if we should introduce LOD for farther trees". godot/World3D/TreeBench.tscn: a 256 world made all forest (a tree on 30% of open tiles), first person in the middle at noon turning a full circle, vsync off; modes lods (the game's) / noshadow / near / mid / far (one LOD everywhere), at tree chunk radius 3, 5, 7 (Trees.cs has bench-only knobs for these). **Numbers** (RTX 5070, 720p): 3,585 trees in range, the game's LODs 0.73 ms GPU, 527 draw calls, 2.1M primitives with shadows; the near LOD everywhere (no LOD) 1.28 ms, 1,002, 5.9M; radius 7 (17,112 trees, ~120 m) 0.79 ms; 1080p 0.91 (no LOD 1.50). **Decided:** the LODs (already in) stay; no impostors or extra far tier: the far LOD is 10-86 triangles with no shadow, so five times the trees adds 0.06 ms. What's left is the near LOD and its shadows (a finer LOD grid if ever needed). Tables in docs/trees.md "Benchmark".
