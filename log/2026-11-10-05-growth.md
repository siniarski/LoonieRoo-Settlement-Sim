- **2026-11-10, foliage grows** (new `cs/Sim/Systems/Growth.cs`, `godot/World3D/Undergrowth.cs`). The player asked for the world to overgrow as the days pass. Built as a pure function plus a sparse record (docs/trees.md "Growth" has the model, the stage table and the options; docs/decisions.md the decision):
  - **The model.** A tile's cover (grass, weeds, bush, crack, creep, sapling progress, each 0..1) is a function of the seed, the tile, its `GrowthKind` (yard, verge, meadow, field, dirt, forest floor, road, pavement, a ruin's floor; from the ground and, in a generated world, the plan's lots and roads) and the growing days since it was cleared. Growing days: spring and summer 1 a day, autumn 0.35, winter 0 (half again in a nuclear winter); a season is 5 game days, so a year is 20 days and 11.75 growing days (0.59 a day). Pace per tile 0.75-1.25. `Growth.G(state, tick)` is closed form, `tickWhen` its inverse (no loops over days). Nothing iterates the map: 65,536 tiles' cover takes 30 ms (0.45 us a tile) when the drawer asks.
  - **Rates (game days from day 1, in spring).** Grass long on yards and verges 1-12; weeds 14-60; bushes (a quarter to two fifths of tiles) 40-220; saplings come up day 50-150 and are sim trees day 80-280, full size 100-390; road, pavement and ruin floors crack day 100-340, grass creeps in from road edges 220-600 and along the middle 510-1020. A week changes lawns, a month yards, a year streets (day 300 shows cracked roads with tufts at the edges, bushes in the yards and young trees in the gardens: experiments/growth/).
  - **The record.** `state.cleared` (JsRecord<double>, tile index -> clearing tick), the only saved field; no schema bump (an older save loads with none; checked). `Growth.clear(state, x, y)` is called by Designations.completeBlueprint (walls, doors, floors, furniture), Commands.setStructure and Farming (plot made, harvested); a clear at tick 0 records nothing. `Growth.expire` (once a day) drops an entry once its tile is through every stage (240 growing days at the slowest pace, roads and floors 600), when "since the world began" gives the same answer, so the record holds only tiles cleared in the last ~year and a half.
  - **Trees (one rule).** A sapling site is a hash of the tile by kind (forest floor 5%, field and garden 4%, meadow and dirt up to 6% by `NatureMod.woodedness` at the edges of woods, verge 0.2%, never yards or roads; not within the start's clearing). `Growth.update` (slow ticks, from Farming.updateFarm in place of `FurnitureMod.regrowTrees`) holds a plan per chunk round the survivors (at most two made a call, 1-2 ms each) and plants due trees in tick, row, column order as `tree` furniture `g<x>.<y>`: choppable (TREE_WOOD) and solid through Trunks. A sapling is drawn small and not solid until 40% of its span, then a tree that keeps growing to full size. Two runs, jumps of any size, and a save and load in between give the same trees (tests). Chopped trees' wood still grows back every 3 days: `Growth.regrowWood` over trees only (it swept all furniture before).
  - **In the game.** `Undergrowth.cs`, called from Trees.Update: per chunk within 2 of yours, MultiMeshes (one buffer write each) of grass tufts, weeds, Trees' bush model, small trees for saplings (Trees' own models) and flat crack strips, tufts leaning to road edges; tinted green to straw by season; at the height `Terrain.Height` reports. Budget: at most 1800 tufts a chunk, 26-60 m ranges, two chunk builds a frame, rebuilt when the growth (in quarter days), season, tree growth or furniture changes. Trees.cs draws grown-from-sapling trees at their size so far.
  - **`--day=D`** (Game.cs, godot/README.md): start at noon of day D, only the clock moves (the saplings due by then are planted before the first frame). Pictures: `experiments/growth/{street,garden}-day{1,30,300}.png` (seed 7, 256: `--at=122,129 --look=45` the street, `--at=98,121 --look=180` a house's garden).
  - **Costs** (1280x720, RTX 5070, debug panel, frame 120, day 1 against day 300; vsync held 60 fps throughout): 3D ms/frame in the garden of a town 0.35 -> 0.73 (render CPU 0.37 -> 0.49, GPU 0.27 -> 0.43), on its street 0.33 -> 0.69 (CPU 0.38 -> 0.54), in a field 0.37 -> 0.84 (CPU 0.44 -> 0.45, GPU 0.60 -> 0.52; the panel's GPU reading is noisy by half a millisecond). About +0.4 ms of 3D time. Trees in view went 164 -> 358 in the town, 368 -> 587 on the street, 700 -> 852 in the field. Sim: `Growth.update` 1.8 us a slow tick, a chunk plan 1-2 ms (a floor tile asking Rooms for the room map cost 10-20 ms until plans skipped roads and floors).
  - **Small additive edits outside the new files:** StateTypes.cs (`cleared`), Farming.cs (update, two clears), FurnitureMod.cs (regrowTrees removed), Designations.cs and Commands.cs (a clear each), Handiwork.cs (the forage chance times `Growth.forageBonus`, up to x1.5 on overgrown ground, 1 on day 0: balance not re-run), Game.cs (`--day`), Trees.cs (growth scale, the undergrowth hook, a `Placement` argument).
  - **Decided.** Growth does nothing to the sim but trees and forage: the sim has no cover, hiding or movement-cost notion to hook (docs/trees.md lists the options). Trampling left (it would need a saved per-step count). Floors are outdoors-only (a complete room stays bare). The far field draws no growth.
  - **Left.** The harness's balance players were not re-run (the forage bonus and new trees change a run slightly after day 1). The sibling fire session should call `Growth.clear(state, x, y)` where something burns out (it regrows from that tick, saplings included). Far trees of land not yet generated don't grow. Tests added: `GrowthTest` (10: calendar, tickWhen, day 0 bare, stages, order, winter, clearing, expiry, roads, forage, commands) and `GrowthWorldTest` (6: a sapling is a tree at the tick the tile says, two runs and a save and load agree, values survive a save, day 1 unchanged, a cleared sapling restarts, wood regrows).
