- **2026-11-11, a car's body is the size of the car.** The cars became models at real size (the sedan 4.2 x 1.7 m, the pickup 5 x 1.9 m) while the sim still collided each as one circle 0.43 m in radius: a car against a wall stood into it. The player asked for the collisions to reflect what is drawn.
  - **Decided** (`cs/Sim/Movement/VehicleBody.cs`, part of `Vehicles`).
    - **The body is a row of circles** along the car's length, each as wide as the car and no further apart than a radius (four for the sedan, five for the pickup): `Motion.fits` already knows how a circle meets walls, edge walls, doors, windows, barricades and the map's rim, so nothing new had to learn the grid. Integer maths in SUB units (rule 10).
    - **Solid to it:** all of that; furniture as its whole tile (all but traps and pits); a tree as its trunk; other cars' bodies (a parked car stops you bumper to bumper). Not people or the dead: it runs those down, now by what its body reaches and not its centre.
    - **Moving:** the whole step if it fits, else each axis on its own (it slides along a wall), else as much of the step as fits (halving, six looks), so the bumper ends at the wall and not a step short.
    - **Turning is about the rear axle**, as a car does: the nose swings, the tail stays. With a pivot at the centre a car alongside a wall could not steer away from it (its tail swung in). A turn that would put the body into something is not made.
    - **A car that does not fit where it stands** (an old save's, parked by its old circle) may still move and turn as long as no more of its circles end up in something solid, so it can get itself out; once out it cannot go back in.
    - **Near a car** is near its body (`gap`, `within`): getting in (`ENTER_REACH` is 290 SUB from the body, where it was 400 from the centre), the boot and bonnet anchors, the driver's door beside the seat. You get out exactly at the door if a body fits there, else on the nearest tile clear of the car. What is in the boot is at the boot (`bootTile`), so you reach it from behind the car, not from its middle.
    - **Parking** (`parkable(p, heading, kind)`): world generation, the expedition truck and the `spawnVehicle` command only put a car where its whole body fits on its heading. Three 256 worlds have 16, 13 and 10 cars where they had 24, 17 and 11. Saved worlds keep their cars where they were.
    - Headlights shine from the nose, not from a metre ahead of the centre. The Colony tab draws a car at its body's size.
  - **Left.** People, the dead and animals still walk through a parked car (pathfinding does not know cars; a body is not an obstacle to walkers). A car slides along a wall without being turned by it. The car's top speed is unchanged (160 SUB a tick: 22 km/h).
