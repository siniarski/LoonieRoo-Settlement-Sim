- **2026-11-04, the control prototype (first cut):** F takes over the selected survivor (F again hands them back). WASD walks in 8 directions (diagonals 1.5x a step, no corner cutting, a blocked diagonal slides along the wall); left click swings at the zombie in front (else any next to you), or shoots one at the clicked tile with a loaded gun; right click or E uses a tile next to you: pick up (into the right slot: weapon, body, feet, cart, tool, else carried), eat (takes EAT_TICKS), search a crate or chop a tree (takes SCAVENGE_WORK); with nothing there, it puts down what you carry. The camera follows you. While controlled a survivor does nothing on their own (no jobs, meals, sleep), but needs drain, zombies come, wounds bleed. Input is sim commands (`takeControl`, `steer` sent only when the held keys change, `swing`, `use`), so it replays exactly. `state.control` (control.ts), schema 49. Movement is still tile to tile: this prototype is there to feel whether that's good enough, or whether the sim needs smooth positions.
