# 2026-11-10: the slow tests made cheaper

The check had grown from ~12 s to 16-20 s (724 tests, 82 s of test time over six processes). Test time is now ~51-55 s and
the check 12-13 s (same machine, same load; baseline measured in a clean worktree of the parent commit, four runs: 16, 17,
18, 20 s). Only `cs/Sim.Tests` changed; nothing in `cs/Sim`.

What cost what: a full-size region's plan (a 1024 x 1024 window) is about a second, `WorldPreview.nature` about 0.8 s at
the preview's 256 px, `Worldgen.createWorld` on a 512 world about 0.2 s. The slow tests were many of those.

| test | before | after | how it still covers the property |
|---|---|---|---|
| world regions > every region the same whatever order they're planned in | 14.5 s | 3 s | 1536 world (2 x 2 regions) not 2560 (3 x 3); three small regions planned in opposite orders on fresh instances, and the corner in the next test. Mutant (a region planned while a neighbour is planned loses a link) fails it |
| world regions > the corner region the same planned first on its own as last after the rest (new) | | 1.4 s | the full-size region alone first vs last after the rest |
| world regions > towns stay apart... (borders) | 7.4 s | 2.6 s | same asserts on 2 x 2 (east and south joins each checked: exactly 2 and 2); shares the world with the corner test |
| settlements > every one stands on land... | 10.5 s | 0.3 s | all 96 combinations kept, but placed on a bare state at the world's real start (placeSettlements reads only seed, size, map); a second test (0.6 s) runs createWorld whole for 8 and checks the bare placement is the same. Mutant (water allowed) fails both |
| world preview > same picture on a worker thread | 5.9 s | 2 s | 1088 (still 2 x 2 regions, 6 stages), two builds side by side, stop case on a 256 world (+ stop mid-way) |
| world preview > a big world shows every region's towns | 2.5 s | 1.6 s | 2112 (3 x 3, the last 64 wide), same "6 of 9" |
| towns > siting by prefix sums places the same towns as the original | 4.1 s | 1.5 s | six seeds up to size 256, two seeds of 384, 512, 700 (the original is O(towns x sites x disc)) |
| terrain > country roads go round lakes | 3.3 s | 1.9 s | the old four 1024 seeds caught a lake-blind road in one (seed 3) only. Now five worlds (512, 768) where it does by a wide margin (58-148 points on a lake against at most 8; limit 40); verified by making lakes cost 1 |
| chunk saves > ... (one region, and several) | 4.0 s | 1.0 + 2.9 s | split in two tests, same asserts (the 1536 world's planning is the second's floor) |

Splits so the shards balance: WorldPreviewTest into three classes, ChunkSaveTest into two, WorldRegionsTest into two
(the order test and one_region; the corner and borders tests). Test count 724 -> 727: round_trip split in two, the
corner test, the whole-world settlements test. None removed.

Not shrunk: InvariantsTest (4.2 s alone, 6 s under load): five seeds of 4000 ticks plus a replay of each, and its last
tests count outcomes across all five seeds in one process, so it can't be split without weakening them. It is now the
check's floor. Also left: HeightsTest 2.8 s, WildlifeTest 2.4, NeighboursTest 2.4, RoomsIncrementalTest 2.2.

Sim hot spots seen: `WorldPreview.nature` ~0.8 s for a 256 x 256 picture of a 1088 world (12 us a pixel: isWater and
isForest per sample); `Worldplan.makeRegion` ~1 s for a 1024 window; `Terrain` of a 1536 world ~0.7 s at first use.

Recommendation for check.sh: the six processes run about 9-10 s each, so the shards are balanced and the floor is now
total test time / 6. `SHARDS=10` (20 cores here) takes it to 10-11 s, `SHARDS=8` 11 s.
