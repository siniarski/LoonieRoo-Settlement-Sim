- **2026-11-10, trunks for tile walkers and cars (a side session).** Task: colonists, survivors and residents who walk tile paths, and cars, still passed through tree trunks (open-work "First person and the look"). **Walkers, chosen: offset `at`, keep the tile path.** `Motion.syncStep(state, e)` (Tick.cs calls it each tick for every non-free mover) takes the step's straight line (centre of `from` to centre of `to` by progress), as before, and then `Trunks.pushOut`s it with BODY_RADIUS: a body crossing or standing on a tree's tile is moved out along the line from the trunk's centre, so it arcs round the trunk and a walker that ends a walk on a tree tile stands beside the trunk. The moved-out spot is used only if `Motion.fits` it (a wall or shut door nearby: the straight line stands, so in a one-tile corridor a trunk is still walked through). Why this and not blocking or costing tree tiles in pathfinding: that changes every path through a wood, walkability, what a haul or a build can reach (a wood could close a gap or trap someone) and the balance figures; this changes only the position, so the step's duration, `pos`, the path, jobs and arrival ticks are untouched, nothing is saved (no schema change), it is stateless (a function of the step and the trees now standing: a felled tree gives the tile's centre back) and the cost is a constant: at most 4 tree lookups per mover per tick, 0.3 us a call measured (a hundred colonists: 30 us a tick). **Cars:** the car already could not enter a tree's tile (`Vehicles.blocked`), so it stopped at the tile's edge with its 0.43 m circle 32 SUB (0.125 m) inside the trunk; `Vehicles.move` now slides with the state (`Motion.slide(state, ...)`, the same as free movers): the circle never enters a trunk, a glancing hit slides round it, head on it loses its speed and crashes as a wall does (the existing crash rule: over CRASH_SPEED it is dented and an event is logged) and can reverse away. Open point unchanged: the car is a 0.43 m circle in the sim and drawn about 1 x 1.9 m, so a car stopped head on at a trunk shows its nose about 0.5 m into it (and its side overlaps a trunk it slides past). **Tests** (TrunksTest.cs, 12 now, 734 in all): a colonist's tile path through a wood never puts the body in a trunk, arrives on the same tick as with no trees, and really strays off the line; ending a walk on a tree tile; standing on a tree tile and the felled tree (centre back); a haul-and-build through trees completes; the stuck-state thresholds (600 ticks on one job, 20 idle with a doable job, invariants every tick) hold over 1,500 ticks in a wood with three colonists; a zombie reaches a colonist standing among trees, on a tree's tile or not; a car at a tree stops against the trunk and reverses away, and passes a tree two tiles off its line. **Balance (world balance, seeds 1-6, main vs this): not identical, and why.** With the offset off, seeds 1 and 2 match main to the digit, so the car change and the rest are neutral.
