- **2026-11-10, organic world generation (schema 77).** The player asked for world generation that feels more organic (after the review: one octave of noise at one scale for lakes and forest, one river at a fixed offset, towns one per 170 x 170 everywhere, circles with grid streets). Saves made before this don't load (generated chunks are made again from the seed: they'd come out different).
  - **Terrain (new, cs/Sim/Systems/Terrain.cs).** The land's large scale, once per world from the seed, on a lattice every 16 tiles (bilinear between), cached (4 worlds), never saved, thread-safe: elevation (fBm, 4 octaves from 640 tiles, domain-warped; a broad valley on natureFor's old river line; a broad rise round the start), moisture (fBm at 480, then ranked within the world so small worlds aren't all dry), settlement (fBm at 600). **Water by drainage:** a priority flood from the map's edge and the sea gives every lattice point the neighbour it drains to and fills hollows to their rims; lakes where the filled level stands over the ground by more than a depth set per world so ~6% of the land is lake (LAKE_SHARE; never shallower than LAKE_MIN); rivers where the flow (rain over the catchment, more where wet) reaches RIVER_FLOW, drawn as quadratic curves through the lattice points (each nudged by a hash), tributaries joining mid-curve, 2 to 9 tiles wide by the log of the flow, banks roughened by a little warp. **Springs** on the old river line near the start (each a river head) keep the start's river: every seed of 128, 256, 512 and 2048 tested has water 38-68 tiles from the start (the old river was 48-72). Per tile, river curves come from a 64-tile bucket grid.
  - **Nature (NatureMod).** `noiseD` (value noise at any point) and `fbm`. Woodedness is half moisture, half warped fBm at 60 tiles; trees by `treeChance` (thin at a wood's edge, 0.08, to 0.38 inside; the odd tree just outside it): Worldgen and the far field (FarField) share it, and `isDirt`, instead of each keeping the rule. Ponds rarer (0.9). `isLake`. Roads: a lake cell costs 60 (a river 14), so roads go round lakes.
  - **Towns.** Sited only where settlement is over the world's wildest quarter (Terrain.wildAt, WILD_SHARE 0.25) and score 0.6 x (settlement - 0.5) more: they gather, with wild country between. Streets reach out to a lumpy outline (0.75-1.3 of the radius by noise) instead of a circle. More cabins in wild country. TownsSpeedTest's copy of the original siting has the same rule.
  - **Coverage** (2048, seeds 42 and 7; before: water 9.0%, forest 31%, trees 0.093/tile, towns 146-147): water 9.4% (lakes 6, rivers 1.3), forest 28%, trees 0.087/tile, towns 145 and 132. 10,240: water 9.9%, forest 29%. 128: every one of 16 seeds has a river (13 before), lakes rare (the start's rise covers most of the map).
  - **Tuning on the way**: the first lakes were 25% of the map and the start stood on an island (the valley and fBm hollows filled whole); a fixed lake depth gave 0-24% by seed, hence the share. Roads from the start bridged a lake on one seed (the start's roads are routed inside one region's window and couldn't go round): the start's broad rise keeps lakes back. Each spring at first fed too little to show; then on 128 maps springs on the outermost lattice row (an outlet) gave nothing: kept two points in.
