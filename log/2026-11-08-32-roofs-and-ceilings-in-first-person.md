- **2026-11-08, roofs and ceilings in first person.** Brief (from the first-person session): proper ceilings and roofs; the old ceiling went over any "boards" or "tiles" ground, which missed rooms with other floors, covered boards outside, and left walls open-topped from outside. New `godot/World3D/Roofs.cs`; WorldMesh now puts a ceiling over `Roofs.Indoor` tiles (its signature includes them). **What's indoors:** (1) a generated building is its plan's footprint: the FLOOR cells of each placement in `Lots.layoutLot`, turned onto the map (after `Buildings.toEdges` the outer walls sit on the footprint's edge). The plan comes from the seed, so the footprint is known whatever the walls are doing; the building counts as standing while at least half (STANDING 0.5) of its outer edges hold a wall, door, window or barricade (or a tile wall). Below that it's a ruin: no roof and no ceiling, so knocked-down walls never leave a floating roof, and a few holes leave it on. 0.6 was tried first and took the roof off an intact-looking house at 59% (seed 42, 256 world, house at 216,149). (2) Anything else `Rooms` calls a room (enclosed floor, at most MAX_ROOM_TILES) is somewhere the player walled in: indoors, under **a flat roof of boards** (over the room and the whole-tile walls round it), all such rooms in one node rebuilt on wall changes. Hand-made maps have no plan, so all their rooms are this kind; so is the start shelter. **Roofs:** per building, its own MeshInstance3D (roofs cross chunk edges), built once (3 a frame) and shown or hidden as it stands or falls. The footprint is cut into rectangles; houses, barns, sheds and churches get a pitched roof per rectangle, ridge along the long side, gable (with gable-end walls and barge boards) or hip (houses, by a hash of the lot), 0.35 m eaves with fascia and soffit, 32 degrees (barns and churches steeper); shops, civic buildings and warehouses get a tarred flat deck with a 0.55 m parapet and coping, the parapet joined into straight runs (per-tile blocks left seams). Muted colours given in sRGB and converted (the vertex colours are linear: the first try at a dark tar came out pale grey). Roofs cast shadows double-sided; under a roof the ambient light eases down to 45%, so rooms are darker by day. A roof never shows inside: its underside starts just above WALL_H, where the ceiling is. **Bounded:** only lots in plan chunks near you are known (`lotsByChunk`, the chunk radius plus 24 m, so ceilings are in before the chunk is built); the standing test and rooms rerun only when walkabilityVersion changes, more of the world is generated, or you cross a chunk; `Roofs.Version` goes in the chunks' change key. Heights: `Roofs.Ground(x, y)` (0 for now) plus WALL_H, in one place for the heightmap. **Debug view:** `--eye=X,Y,H` looks from a point instead of your eyes. Screenshots looked at (seed 42: the start shelter from above, a hip-roofed house from the ground and above, inside its rooms facing a window and looking up, a gable house, Hollin's street with the grocery and petrol station, a ruin left open). The worktree needed `godot --headless --path godot --import` before `--shot` would load the character models. Not done: roofs on settlements' own walls beyond what Rooms encloses, dormers or chimneys, and roof textures.
