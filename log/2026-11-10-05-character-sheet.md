- **2026-11-10, the character sheet (left column of the Tab screen).** The player asked for a health and status summary in the Tab menu: who you are and what state you are in, at a glance. The sim already kept all of it, scattered; now `cs/Sim/Systems/CharacterSheet.cs` gathers it into data and text, and `godot/Ui/CharacterSheet.cs` draws it.
  - **The pure part.** `CharacterSheet.build(state, survivor)` returns a `Sheet`: sections (identity, health, conditions, body, needs, mood, skills, wearing), rows with a severity (`Sev`: fine, note, warn, bad, critical), a 0..1 bar where it is a bar, and for conditions what it is doing and what would help; the body by part (head, torso, each arm, each leg) with notes and severity; `CharacterSheet.text(sheet)` (also `textOfControlled(state)`) is the plain text. Every word and colour comes from the sim's own constants (Health.HEALTH_HURT and HEALTH_CRITICAL, BodyMod.COLD, FREEZING, SOAKED, DRENCHED, WINDED, PANICKING, CALM, Food.HUNGRY, Water.THIRSTY, Rest.TIRED, Mood.MISERABLE and MOOD_BASE, Wellbeing.LOW and HIGH, Resolve.BREAKING and STEELED, Wounds.BLEED_EVERY, the amputation window, the garments' life); the tests assert at the boundaries against those constants. Read-only: a test checks the state's JSON is unchanged.
  - **Where the sim keeps less than the task wanted** (the sheet says less rather than invent): (1) a wound has no side, and a bite none until it is cut off: the sheet puts it on the limb that is left if the other is gone, else on a side by a hash of the survivor and the wound's end tick (stable while it lasts; the amputation picks its side by a hash too). (2) No record of a bandage: a wound that is not bleeding reads "not bleeding (bandaged, or it stopped by itself)". (3) Head and torso have no wounds; the torso carries the overall health word. (4) "Dying" is critical health (`Health.isCritical`) with something still taking health (bleeding, starving, dehydrated, freezing, sick, infected): the sim has no thresholds beyond hurt (< 600) and critical (< 300). (5) Pain is not modelled, so it is not listed; there is one wound at a time. (6) Days survived is the world's day minus one (a survivor's own arrival is not kept). (7) Limb levels are the ones the character lab knows: wrist, elbow, shoulder, ankle, knee, hip; the sim only makes elbow and knee today.
  - **The panel.** `CharacterSheetPanel` (godot/Ui/CharacterSheet.cs, 320 px wide at x 8): name, age, background, days; the health bar and word; a body outline drawn in code (parts tinted by severity, a missing limb as a faint ghost with its stump at the level; hover for the notes) beside the conditions (name in its colour, what it does, "→" what helps); then the body parts with something to say, body, needs, mood and its thoughts with weights, wearing and wielding with the numbers the sim has, skills with progress, and who you are. It scrolls. It rebuilds when the sheet's text changes, at most five times a second, keeping the scroll, and follows the survivor you control. Pictures: `experiments/character-sheet/sheet-healthy.png` and `sheet-hurt.png` (the column), `-screen.png` (the whole screen, half size).
  - **Edits in shared files.** `godot/Ui/Inventory.cs`: a field, `AddChild(sheet)` in the constructor, `sheet.Update(state, s)` in Update, and `outer.OffsetLeft = outer.OffsetRight = CharacterSheetPanel.WIDTH / 2;` to move the two columns right of the sheet (with a shelf open they ran under it, and the self-test's drag-out point landed on the sheet); the grid-inventory rebuild reserves its own column, so that last line is the merge's to drop. `godot/SelfTest.cs`: one line, `await SheetSteps();`. Step in `godot/SelfTestSheet.cs`: Tab shows the sheet, it is you and fine, it fits and leaves the bag clear, hurting you in the sim turns it to dying with the conditions worst first, putting it back puts it back; `--sheetshot=prefix` (with `--selftest`) saves the pictures.
  - **Left.** Nothing about wounds by place (head, torso) or pain exists in the sim to show. The panel is not tuned for a window smaller than 1280x720 (the column is a fixed 320 px; the content scrolls). If the sim ever tracks several wounds, bandages or pain, `CharacterSheet.cs` is the one place to extend.
