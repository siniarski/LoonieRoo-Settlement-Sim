- **2026-11-11, textures: living things and plants.** The player asked for textures on everything; after the world's surfaces (log/2026-11-11-12-textures-surfaces.md) this session put the kit's detail on people, the dead, animals and trees, where it helps. How a body picks its layer: docs/characters.md "Detail on bodies"; the kit: docs/textures.md.
  - **What the meshes carried.** The pz look models (everyone the game draws) have one smart-UV atlas (a 512 painted map), no UV2 and no rest position the shader can read (Godot skins before `vertex()`); the dead also have a recolour mask, but only of their clean cloth. The animals had vertex colours and no UVs at all. So: people by their own atlas UV, animals by a new UV2.
  - **People and the dead.** `art/scripts/build_detail_maps.py` (new, seconds, no rebuild of the characters) writes `<file>_detail.png` beside each of the 11 look files: it lays the near LOD's triangles into the atlas by their UV with their rest positions and asks the paint's own regions (paint.regions): red the painted shirt and trousers (less the dead's holes) and every garment piece, green the painted shoes and boots; and `<file>_detail.json`, the atlas's metres a UV unit (2.0-2.8, area-weighted). The dead's recolour shader (Characters/recolour.gdshaderinc) multiplies in the kit's cloth under red and rubber under green, faded out from 2 to 5 m; the living now wear that shader too, in their file's own colours (Looks.Recolour with no mask: one material per file and surface). The look lab dresses its single pz subjects the same way, so its pictures are the game's.
    - Scale: at the cloth layer's own 0.5 m the weave reads at arm's length, at 0.22 m it vanished even at 1.2 m (the weave is a few texels a thread: finer than a pixel); 0.4 m kept. Strength 1 (0.8 hardly showed over the painted map).
  - **Animals.** build_animals.py `detail_uvs`: per face, UV = (-(fur + 1), 1 - repeats) (none on eyes and antlers) and UV2 = the rest position along the face's main axis, so strands run down the flanks and chest and back along the spine. Fur every 0.18 m on a rabbit up to 0.5 m on a bear. The glb grows (68 to 113 kB for the rabbit: vertices split where the projection changes). Animals.cs puts one shader on every coat (vertex colour times SURFACE_DETAIL; checked against the file's StandardMaterial3D: the same colours to 0.003).
  - **Trees.** The bark material's two 3D noise furrows (16 hashes a fragment) became the kit's bark laid by the unswayed object position, 1.5 repeats a metre, times 0.9 (the noise's mean, so a trunk keeps its tone past the fade). Bushes and saplings wear the same material.
  - **Left plain, on purpose.** Skin, faces, hair, eyes, teeth, hair meshes, stump caps; grass tufts and weeds (blades a few pixels wide), foliage masses (the leaves have their own texture); the burnt stumps (Fires.cs: near black). There are no felled stumps or logs in the world. The mannequin, mid-tier and VAT shaders are labs only (the game's crowd is People's 40 models and capsules), so untouched.
