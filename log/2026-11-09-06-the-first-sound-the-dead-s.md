- **2026-11-09, the first sound: the dead's moans and blows landing.** Brief (from the first-person session): the game's first sound, positional in first person, with a volume setting. **The plan (decided with the player):** the first sounds are the dead's (a snarl when one sees you, groans from the near ones now and then) and a blow landing (a thud, a heavier one for the killing blow); **made by our own code, no downloads and no audio files in git**; a file in `res://assets/sounds/` named `<kind>_<n>.ogg|wav` (kinds `moan_idle`, `moan_notice`, `hit`, `kill`) replaces the made ones of its kind, so a CC0 pack can drop in later with the player's OK. **How it reads the sim (rule 5):** nothing added to the sim. `Noise.makeNoise` stores nothing, so it isn't the hook; instead `godot/Audio/Sounds.cs` compares what it sees each frame with the last: a zombie's `targetId` turning to you (notice), its `hurtTick` moving (hit), a zombie gone within 3 m of someone whose `kills` went up (kill, where it stood); idle groans roll once a second per zombie within 24 m (`Hash.hashRoll` of its id and the second: 4%, 12% while it hunts someone). A jump of more than 30 ticks, a load or going backwards is a fresh start, not events (coming back from the map doesn't replay what happened meanwhile). **Playing:** 10 `AudioStreamPlayer3D` voices (inverse-distance, unit 4 m, silent past 40 m, a low-pass with distance), each following the body it speaks for; a busy pool gives way by priority (hit and kill over notice over idle); pitch varies ±8% by hash; the camera is the listener. `godot/Audio/Synth.cs`: a moan is a pulse built harmonic by harmonic (up to 4.5 kHz, 22,050 Hz mono) shaped by three formants drifting between two of four vowels, a pitch that rises then sags (85-115 Hz down to 55-72; the snarl 120-160 down to 70-90, a sharp attack), jitter, a slow wobble, vocal fry and breath; a hit is a falling thump, filtered noise and a crack. 6 idle moans, 4 snarls, 3 hits, 2 killing blows, seeded, the same every run. `godot/Audio/Volume.cs`: the volume (0-1, the master bus at the square, muted at 0) in `user://settings.cfg`, a slider on the menu's main page; a setting of the machine, never in state or saves. Hooks: FirstPerson.cs (3 lines), Menu.cs (the slider), SelfTest.cs; Game.cs untouched. **Checked:** `./check.sh --all` green; the self-test adds 5 steps (27 in all): the slider sets the bus (40 -> -15.9 dB) and is put back; the made sounds aren't silent (and are written to `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\LoonieRoo\sounds-preview\*.wav` to listen to); a zombie put two tiles in front of you takes you as its target and the snarl plays from it (0.1 m off); a blow and the killing blow each play. Spectrograms of the moans looked at: harmonics sagging in pitch under formant bands; the first try buried the upper harmonics under breath (tilt 1/k^0.9 -> 1/k^0.65, breath down by a quarter). **Not verified by ear:** nobody has listened to any of it; whether the moans read as the dead (or as a hum and hiss) is the player's call, and the voices' falloff and loudness want tuning by ear. Left: walls and doors don't muffle (no occlusion), no sound outside first person (the Colony tab and map are silent), your own footsteps and swing, the rest of `Noise`'s kinds (gunfire, glass, alarms, engines), ambience (wind, rain, night), the synthesis cost at startup not measured (15 sounds, est. a fraction of a second).
