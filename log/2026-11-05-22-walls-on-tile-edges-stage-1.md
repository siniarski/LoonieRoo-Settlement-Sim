- **2026-11-05, walls on tile edges: stage 1, the foundation (branch edge-walls).** Player chose walls as the borders of tiles (as in Project Zomboid and The Sims) over walls that fill a tile, on a branch, starting now, isometric after. Planned in stages so the suite stays green throughout: tile walls keep working alongside edge walls (so the 126 test maps written with `#` and `D` don't need rewriting), and each stage moves more of the game over.
  - Stage 1 (this): grid.ts gains `edges` (two per tile: its north and its west side; EDGE_NONE, EDGE_WALL or EDGE_DOOR; the map's own border needs none), `edgeOpen` and `edgePassing` (edge doors standing open, and held open for someone passing), with edgeIndex, edgeBetween, setEdge; saved (run-length encoded like the other layers; schema bumped). pathfinding.ts `crossable(a -> b, mover)`: an edge wall stops everyone (breachers too: edges can't be battered yet); an edge door follows the door-tile rules (colonists and raiders open it, you and the dead need it open). A*, the reachability components, canReach and straightened walking (no diagonal round an edged corner; edge doors crossed straight) all go through it. motion.ts `fits`: a body's circle mustn't reach an edge wall's line (or a door it can't pass), so you slide along edge walls. rooms.ts: edge walls bound a room; an edge door does too and counts as its door. Lines of fire (clearShot) and the dead's straight-line close-in (clearLine) are blocked by edge walls and doors (a diagonal step can pass either way round the corner): work.ts seesAcross. doors.ts: colonists and raiders open an edge door as they step across, and it shuts once nobody is crossing or standing in the doorway; you open or shut one by using the tile across it (control.ts use). draw.ts: an edge wall is a bar a fifth of a tile thick along the border, a door a wooden leaf between short jambs, swung back along the frame when open. Scenarios take `edges: [{ x, y, side, kind }]`. tests/edges.test.ts. 682 tests.
  - Next stages: 2) generated buildings (buildings.ts plans) built with edge walls and doors, so rooms are the full size of their floors; 3) building, repairing, barricading, locking, breaching (zombies battering an edge), fire, damage and the build tools on edges; 4) the minimap (a tile with an edge wall takes the wall's colour), the ASCII text renderer, and whether tile walls stay (rubble, fences) or go.
