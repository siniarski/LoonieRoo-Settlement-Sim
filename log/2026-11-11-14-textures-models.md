- **2026-11-11, textures: the models.** The player asked for textures on everything; after the world's surfaces (log/2026-11-11-12-textures-surfaces.md) this session gave the kit's detail layers to every model in the world: furniture, items on the ground and in the hand, the sedan and the pickup outside and the driver's cabins, crops and soil, building sites, the helicopter, the wagon and the stall. Nothing in the sim; no new layer.
  - **How a model picks its layer: its colour's material**, in the Blender script. `art/scripts/lowpoly.py` now has `MATERIALS` (name: layer and repeats per metre), `mat(name, colour)`, `plain(colour)` and `write_surfaces`; a colour that is a material keeps it through `mul`. A plain colour with metalness wears painted metal, without none (labels, lamps, food, leaves); glass, chrome, mirrors, screens and the radio's display are `plain`. build_furniture.py and build_items.py keep their own shape helpers and take mul, the materials and write_surfaces from lowpoly; build_world.py and build_cabins.py already used it. docs/textures.md "The models" is the page.
    - Materials given: wood (planks 1.2/m: chairs, frames, legs, bookcases, furniture's handles), rough (planks 0.7: crates, pallets, the items' planks and handles, sites' lumber, the wagon), painted (metal 1.2: kitchen counters, the sink unit), metal (1.5: steel, rust, enamel, the fridge and icebox, tins, the wheelbarrow's tray; and every plain colour with metalness: stoves, cabinets, lockers, tools, guns), body (metal 0.8: the sedan, the pickup, the tractor, the cabins' bonnets and mirrors' housings, the helicopter), rubber (3: tyres, a boot's sole, wellingtons, the wheelbarrow's wheel), plastic (plaster 3, a faint mottle: the cabins' dashboard and trim, a bin, the desk's monitor), cloth (1.2: sofas, armchairs, mattresses, blankets, pillows, the cot, sacks, sandbags, tarps, folded cloth and coats, the cabins' seats and carpet), fine (cloth 3: rope, twine, the flag, the headlining, the sleeve), leather (cloth 1.5: the leather sofa, a suitcase, boots, the glove), earth (1.5: farm beds, the pit), straw (fur 2: hay), concrete (stone and concrete), brick, paper (grime 2: cardboard boxes).
  - **Laid by the mesh's own coordinates, not by position.** Each face's coordinates in metres go into a second UV map (UV2) and its UV says `-(layer + 1)`: the detail stays on a car that drives and an item that is carried with no per-instance work, any mesh orientation reads the same, and sloped faces (a windscreen pillar, a chair's back) are not stretched as the by-position projection would. Each face's axes: up the picture is up a face that stands; for boards, along the face's longest side (a leg's boards run up it, a shelf's along it, a crate's slats along the slat). Boards drawn 2.5 times longer along the grain (`ALONG = 0.4`): the planks picture's butt joints every ~28 cm cut a lone plank into pieces (the first pictures); now ~70 cm apart. Cost: 8 bytes a vertex.
  - **Game:** ModelSet's, Furniture's and Items' shaders include the kit (`SURFACE_BY_OBJECT`, `ALBEDO = COLOR.rgb * SURFACE_DETAIL`), made with `Surfaces.Shader`; metalness and roughness from the alpha as before. Cars, Cockpit, Held, Fields, Sites, Aircraft and Others draw through them. The dashboard's dials are Cockpit's own panel material, untouched. A face with UV 0 draws exactly as before.
