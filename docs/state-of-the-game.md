# The state of the game

As of 2026-11-12, for someone reading cold. Each line was checked against the code (the key is in
docs/controls.md; the module in docs/modules.md). Nothing here has had a long human playtest: "works" means the sim's
tests and the self-test pass, not that it feels right.

## What you can do today

- **Start.** Main menu, New game, "Who you are" (character creation: name, look, backstory). A world is generated by
  region from a seed (organic ground, rivers, lakes, woods, towns with streets, lots and real buildings); you begin in
  first person in a shelter on the edge of a town. Three save slots and a dawn autosave; "Report a bug" writes a
  replayable file.
- **Move.** Mouse look, WASD, Shift to run, C to sneak, Space to jump, vault a broken window, barricade or fence, or hop
  one tile of water. Walls and doors stand on tile edges; doors open and shut (E); locking is a Colony-tab order.
- **Swim.** Rivers and lakes: wade at 60% pace in the shallows, swim further out (no running, hands busy, endurance
  drains; Space is a stroke).
- **Fight.** Click swings (knife, hatchet, machete, crowbar, bat, pipe, sledge, spear, plank; bare hands punch); pistol,
  revolver, shotgun, rifle and crossbow fire where you look (X reload, Q next weapon, right mouse aims down the sights).
  Sneak attacks on those who haven't seen you. Wounds, bleeding, infection, amputation, herbs and a medkit; the
  character sheet (Tab) shows the body by part.
- **Carry.** The bag is a grid (8 x 6, 12 kg), with pockets, containers (crates, shelves, fridges, boots) and the ground
  as grids; drag, turn, split, right-click menus; three quick slots on 1-3.
- **Survive.** Hunger, thirst, rest, body heat, wetness, endurance, panic, infection, mood and thoughts; forage on
  grass by day, hunt, cook, sleep; night, rain, snow (it lies and you leave a trail), heatwaves and grass fires.
- **The dead.** Zombies from day one, more each night; shamblers, runners and crawlers, woken by noise and sight; they
  climb through windows, over low fences and cars, batter barricades and come in hordes. Animals (deer, wolves, dogs).
- **Drive and use cars.** Get in from any side (E), drive (W S A D), dashboard (speed, fuel, temperature, lamps),
  headlights (G), the boot (U) and bonnet (I), fuel from a can; push a parked car (J); climb on a roof or hide under a
  pickup (C then E); a dent can set off its alarm.
- **Build and board up.** R with wood at a door or window boards it, repairs, reinforces; walls and furniture via the
  Colony tab's build bar. Lights have switches (Z), the flashlight is G; rooms need power (mains or a generator).
- **Run a colony** (the map screen's Colony tab: B, P, O). Designate walls, doors, rooms (housing, mess, infirmary,
  storage), farms, crafting and hunting; survivors take jobs by priority and shift, sleep in assigned beds, eat in the
  mess, get mood from comfort and company, are treated by a doctor, guard at night. You can play any one of them (F).
- **Deal with others.** Talk (T) to neighbours' people, who ask for errands; wanderers ask to join; traders and raiders
  arrive; prisoners can be recruited; neighbouring settlements trade, send caravans and scavenging parties, hold
  alliances and feuds, and run expeditions and outposts; the radio. A map screen (M) with notes, minimap (N).
- **Tell people.** V follow me, H hold, L loot that room.

## Known not to work, or not finished (most important first)

1. **Nobody has played most of it.** Cars, swimming, weapons' feel, lights and the colony's winter are untested by a
   human beyond two short play lists (docs/open-work.md "Not played yet").
2. **Solo survival is probably too hard**: the scripted solo player dies by day 2-5; colonies die in the first winter
   without coops or a greenhouse. Balance is unproven (NOTES.md "Open questions").
3. **Big worlds are not scaled.** Chunks load but are never unloaded; loading is regeneration; the target of ~100,000
   dead on ~10,000 x 10,000 is not reached (docs/open-work.md "Performance and big worlds").
4. **No storeys, basements or slopes in the sim**; the ground has relief for the eye only.
5. **Residents are simple.** Neighbours potter, trade and fight; colonists never use bag grids or light switches.
6. **Rough in the view and ears**: textures are a thin detail layer, lights have no bloom, rain has sound but fire and
   most weapon actions have none; water is a flat sheet; no swimming pose; cars have no hands on the wheel.
7. **Animation gaps**: the dead's feet still slide a little at stops, there is no ragdoll in the world, no vault clip.
8. **The K help line** is behind (docs/controls.md ends with what it omits).
9. Worlds saved before world-generation version 3 (now) are refused.
